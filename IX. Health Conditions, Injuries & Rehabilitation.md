## IX. Health Conditions, Injuries & Rehabilitation

This section organizes clinical-facing training guidance for injury prevention,
acute management, rehabilitation, and return-to-function decisions. It connects
contraindications, pain science, and progression logic so exercise remains
therapeutic rather than aggravating. Use these notes when safety constraints,
symptom variability, or medical history must shape load selection and training
progression.
