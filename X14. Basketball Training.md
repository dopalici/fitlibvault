### X14. Basketball Training
**ID:** X14-BASKETBALL **Created:** 2025-06-05

Basketball training integrates explosive power, agility, endurance, and basketball-specific movement patterns to optimize performance in jumping, cutting, shooting, and sustained high-intensity play. Training emphasizes vertical power development, lateral movement, and anaerobic conditioning for intermittent high-intensity efforts.

**Core Components:**
- **Vertical Jump Development:** Plyometric progressions ([[X12. Vertical Jump Training]]), depth jumps, and Olympic lifting variations ([[B15. Olympic Weightlifting]]) to maximize vertical leap for rebounding, shot blocking, and layups ([[A32. Power Profiling and Testing]]).
- **Lateral Agility and Cutting:** Multi-directional movement drills, lateral bounds, and change-of-direction training to improve defensive slides, cutting ability, and injury prevention ([[L3. Balance and Coordination Training]]).
- **Anaerobic Power and Conditioning:** High-intensity interval training ([[E3. High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)]]) that mimics game demands with work-to-rest ratios approximating basketball play patterns ([[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]]).
- **Upper Body Power for Shooting:** Medicine ball throws, plyometric push-ups ([[EXL-83 Push-up (Standard & Variations)]]), and shooting-specific strength exercises to improve shot power and accuracy ([[B4. Strength Training Principles]]).
- **Core Stability and Anti-Rotation:** Emphasis on maintaining stable shooting platform and injury prevention during contact ([[L4. Core Stability Training]]) using exercises like Pallof press ([[EXL-77 Cable Pallof Press (Anti-Rotation)]]).
