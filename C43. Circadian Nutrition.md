### C43. Circadian Nutrition

**ID:** C43-CIRCADIAN-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Circadian nutrition involves aligning food intake timing and composition with natural biological rhythms to optimize metabolism, performance, and recovery. This approach leverages chronobiology research to understand how meal timing affects hormonal responses, nutrient utilization, and metabolic efficiency throughout the 24-hour cycle.

**Core Components:**

- **Meal Timing Optimization:** Strategically timing meals and macronutrients to align with circadian metabolic patterns and insulin sensitivity fluctuations ([[C3. Nutrient Timing]], [[A10. Hormonal Responses to Exercise]])
- **Sleep-Nutrition Interface:** Understanding how nutrition timing affects sleep quality, melatonin production, and overnight recovery processes ([[F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]], [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]])
- **Training Schedule Integration:** Aligning nutritional strategies with training times to optimize performance and adaptation based on circadian hormone fluctuations ([[C5. Pre, During, and Post-Exercise Nutrition]], [[A37. Hormonal Adaptations to Training]])
- **Shift Work Applications:** Developing specialized nutrition protocols for athletes with irregular schedules or those training across time zones ([[C45. Travel Nutrition]], [[G2. Age-Specific Exercise Recommendations]])
- **Metabolic Synchronization:** Using light exposure, meal timing, and specific nutrients to maintain or reset circadian rhythms for optimal metabolic function ([[A35. Metabolic Adaptations to Training]], [[C14. Intermittent Fasting (IF)]])
