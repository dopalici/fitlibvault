### Fatigue Assessment Protocols
**ID:** F19 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Fatigue assessment protocols provide systematic methods for quantifying and monitoring different types of fatigue, including central nervous system fatigue, peripheral muscular fatigue, and metabolic fatigue. These assessments are crucial for understanding training stress accumulation and preventing performance decrements associated with inadequate recovery.

**Core Components:**
- **Central Nervous System Fatigue Testing:** Assessment of neural drive and voluntary activation using electrical stimulation techniques and maximal voluntary contractions
- **Peripheral Fatigue Evaluation:** Measurement of muscular contractile properties, force-generating capacity, and [[A7. Muscle Physiology Fundamentals]] responses to fatigue
- **Metabolic Fatigue Indicators:** Analysis of lactate accumulation, pH changes, and substrate utilization patterns reflecting [[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]] stress
- **Cognitive Fatigue Assessment:** Evaluation of reaction time, decision-making capacity, and attention span affected by training load and [[M2. Overtraining and Functional Overreaching]]
- **Progressive Fatigue Testing:** Protocols measuring fatigue development over extended exercise durations incorporating [[E1. Aerobic vs. Anaerobic Training]] demands
