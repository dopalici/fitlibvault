## H. Body Composition

This section translates body-composition goals into measurable, realistic
planning. It ties assessment methods, expected rates of change, and training
nutrition coordination into one decision loop so users avoid chasing impossible
timelines. Use it for setting evidence-based expectations and adjusting plans
without overreacting to short-term scale noise.
