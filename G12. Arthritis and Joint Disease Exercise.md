### Arthritis and Joint Disease Exercise
**ID:** G12 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Arthritis and joint disease exercise encompasses therapeutic exercise interventions designed to reduce pain, improve joint mobility, and maintain functional capacity in individuals with various forms of arthritis including osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and other inflammatory joint conditions. This approach balances joint protection with progressive loading to optimize outcomes while minimizing disease flares.

- **Strength Training Adaptations:** Modified resistance training protocols that strengthen periarticular muscles without exacerbating joint symptoms, relating to [[B4. Strength Training Principles]] and [[A43. Muscular Adaptations to Training]]
