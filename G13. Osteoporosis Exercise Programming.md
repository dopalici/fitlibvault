### Osteoporosis Exercise Programming
**ID:** G13 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Osteoporosis exercise programming focuses on evidence-based exercise interventions to improve bone mineral density, reduce fracture risk, and maintain functional capacity in individuals with osteoporosis or osteopenia. This approach emphasizes weight-bearing and resistance exercises while incorporating fall prevention strategies and spine protection protocols.

**Core Components:**
- **Bone Loading Principles:** Understanding how mechanical stress stimulates bone formation through weight-bearing and resistance exercises, connecting to [[A41. Skeletal Adaptations to Training]] and [[A19. Principle of Overload]]
- **Weight-Bearing Exercise Protocols:** Specific recommendations for impact and weight-bearing activities that promote bone health, linking to [[B4. Strength Training Principles]] and [[X1. Running (Track, Road, Trail)]]
- **Spine Protection Guidelines:** Exercise modifications and contraindications to prevent vertebral compression fractures, relating to [[A18. Proper Lifting Mechanics]] and [[IX-6. Exercise Contraindications]]
- **Fall Prevention Training:** Balance and stability interventions to reduce fall risk and subsequent fracture potential, connecting to [[L3. Balance and Coordination Training]] and [[IX-2. Injury Prevention Strategies]]
- **Nutritional Support Integration:** Understanding calcium, vitamin D, and other nutritional factors that support bone health alongside exercise, linking to [[A4. Micronutrients]] and [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]]
