## F. Optimal Performance Factors

This section covers the variables that most strongly determine day-to-day and
season-long performance outcomes. It links sleep, psychology, recovery, injury
risk, and testing systems into one operating model so training decisions are
based on readiness, not guesswork. Use it to improve consistency, reduce
avoidable setbacks, and align adaptation goals with athlete context.
