## L. Cross-Training and Functional Fitness

This section organizes movement-quality work that supports performance across
modalities, not just one sport or one lift. It combines patterning, mobility,
coordination, and trunk control so users can build resilient movement capacity
and transfer skills across contexts. Use these notes to close weak links that
limit force production, movement efficiency, or injury resilience.
