### N6. Senior Fitness Programming
**ID:** N6-SENIOR-FITNESS-PROGRAMMING **Created:** 2025-06-05

Specialized exercise programming for older adults (65+) focusing on functional independence, fall prevention, and quality of life maintenance. These programs emphasize multi-component training approaches that address age-related physiological changes while promoting safe, sustainable activity participation.

**Core Components:**
- **Functional Movement Preservation:** Exercise selection prioritizing activities of daily living through [[L1. Movement Patterns and Functional Training]] and [[L3. Balance and Coordination Training]]
- **Sarcopenia Prevention:** Resistance training protocols targeting muscle mass preservation using [[B4. Strength Training Principles]] and [[B2. Protein Requirements for Muscle Growth]]
- **Fall Risk Reduction:** Balance and coordination training incorporating [[L3. Balance and Coordination Training]] and proprioceptive challenges for injury prevention
- **Cardiovascular Health Maintenance:** Aerobic programming considering age-related changes through [[E2. Steady-State Cardio (LISS - Low-Intensity Steady State)]] and [[A8. Cardiovascular Physiology Fundamentals]]
- **Chronic Condition Management:** Exercise prescription modifications for common health conditions using [[G3. Training with Health Limitations  Chronic Conditions]] principles
