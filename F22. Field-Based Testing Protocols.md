### Field-Based Testing Protocols
**ID:** F22 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Field-based testing protocols provide practical and accessible assessment methods that can be implemented outside laboratory settings while maintaining scientific validity and reliability. These protocols enable widespread testing across various populations and environments, making performance assessment more accessible and sport-specific.

**Core Components:**
- **Submaximal Fitness Testing:** Validated field tests including step tests, walk tests, and submaximal cycling protocols that estimate fitness levels without requiring maximal effort
- **Sprint and Agility Assessments:** Standardized protocols for measuring speed, acceleration, and change of direction abilities relevant to [[X11. Sprinting]] and team sports
- **Functional Movement Testing:** Field-appropriate versions of [[A17. Movement Screening]] and [[L1. Movement Patterns and Functional Training]] assessments
- **Sport-Specific Field Tests:** Tailored protocols reflecting the demands of specific sports including [[X4. Team Sports - General Considerations]] and individual activities
- **Portable Technology Integration:** Use of [[VIII-3. Wearable Technology (Fitness Trackers, Smartwatches, HRMs)]] and mobile assessment tools for accurate field measurements
