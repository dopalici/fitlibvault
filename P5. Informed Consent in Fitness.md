### P5. Informed Consent in Fitness
**ID:** P5-INFORMED-CONSENT **Created:** 2025-06-05

Informed consent in fitness involves providing clients with comprehensive information about exercise programs, risks, benefits, and alternatives to enable autonomous decision-making about their participation. This ethical and legal principle ensures clients understand what they are agreeing to and can make educated choices about their fitness journey.

**Core Components:**
- **Risk Disclosure:** Clear communication of potential risks associated with exercise participation, including injury risk, cardiovascular demands, and contraindications ([[IX-6. Exercise Contraindications]], [[A8. Cardiovascular Physiology Fundamentals]], [[IX-1. Common Musculoskeletal Injuries]])
- **Program Explanation:** Detailed description of exercise protocols, training methodologies, and expected outcomes, including realistic timelines for results ([[B1. Progressive Overload]], [[Q1. Exercise Progression Principles]], [[H2. Realistic Muscle Gain Rates]])
- **Alternative Options:** Presentation of different training approaches and modification possibilities to accommodate individual needs and preferences ([[G2. Age-Specific Exercise Recommendations]], [[G3. Training with Health Limitations & Chronic Conditions]], [[L1. Movement Patterns and Functional Training]])
- **Documentation Requirements:** Proper written consent forms, health screening questionnaires, and ongoing consent verification processes ([[P1. Ethical Training and Coaching]], [[P4. Evidence-Based Coaching Methodologies]])
- **Ongoing Communication:** Continuous dialogue about program adjustments, client comfort levels, and emerging concerns throughout the training relationship ([[F4. Behavioral Psychology in Fitness]], [[P6. Professional Boundaries and Relationships]])
