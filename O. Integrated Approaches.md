## O. Integrated Approaches

This section combines training, nutrition, recovery, and monitoring into one
coordinated system. Instead of optimizing each pillar in isolation, it focuses
on trade-offs, sequencing, and feedback loops that determine real-world
outcomes. Use it when individual plans look good on paper but fail in practice
because load, fueling, and lifestyle constraints are not aligned.
