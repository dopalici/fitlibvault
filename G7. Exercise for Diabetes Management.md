### Exercise for Diabetes Management
**ID:** G7 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Exercise for diabetes management encompasses evidence-based physical activity interventions that improve glycemic control, insulin sensitivity, and cardiovascular health in individuals with Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes. This approach integrates exercise prescription with blood glucose monitoring, medication timing, and nutritional considerations for optimal metabolic outcomes.

- **Exercise Prescription Guidelines:** Specific recommendations for aerobic and resistance training frequency, intensity, and duration for diabetic populations, linking to [[A5. FITT Principle]] and [[E1. Aerobic vs. Anaerobic Training]]
- **Blood Glucose Monitoring:** Pre-, during, and post-exercise glucose management strategies to prevent hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia, relating to [[Q4. Interpreting Biometric & Progress Data]] and [[IX-6. Exercise Contraindications]]
- **Cardiovascular Risk Management:** Addressing heightened cardiovascular disease risk through appropriate exercise selection and monitoring, connecting to [[A39. Cardiovascular Adaptations to Training]] and [[E13. Heart Rate Zone Training]]
- **Nutritional Timing Coordination:** Integrating exercise with meal timing and carbohydrate intake for optimal metabolic control, linking to [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] and [[C5. Pre, During, and Post-Exercise Nutrition]]
