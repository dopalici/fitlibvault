### X18. Volleyball Training
**ID:** X18-VOLLEYBALL **Created:** 2025-06-05

Volleyball training emphasizes vertical jump development, shoulder stability for overhead athletes, and reactive agility for net play and defensive movements. Training focuses on explosive leg power, rotational core strength, and injury prevention for repetitive jumping and overhead motions.

**Core Components:**
- **Vertical Jump and Approach Training:** Plyometric progressions ([[X12. Vertical Jump Training]]), depth jumps, and approach-specific jump training to maximize spike and block height while improving landing mechanics ([[A32. Power Profiling and Testing]]).
- **Shoulder Health and Overhead Power:** Rotator cuff strengthening, scapular stability exercises, and progressive overhead throwing patterns to prevent overuse injuries in spiking and serving ([[IX-2. Injury Prevention Strategies]]).
- **Lateral Movement and Court Coverage:** Multi-directional agility drills, lateral bounds, and reaction training to improve defensive positioning and diving/rolling techniques ([[L3. Balance and Coordination Training]]).
- **Rotational Core Power for Attacking:** Medicine ball throws, cable rotations ([[EXL-76 Cable Woodchop (High-to-Low & Low-to-High)]]), and anti-rotation exercises ([[L4. Core Stability Training]]) to generate power in spiking while maintaining trunk stability during play.
- **Lower Body Power and Landing Mechanics:** Emphasis on eccentric strength ([[B20. Eccentric Training]]) and proper landing patterns to reduce ACL injury risk common in jumping sports, combined with unilateral leg strength training ([[EXL-36 Dumbbell Lunge (Static/Alternating/Walking)]]).
