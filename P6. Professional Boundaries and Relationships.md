### P6. Professional Boundaries and Relationships
**ID:** P6-PROFESSIONAL-BOUNDARIES **Created:** 2025-06-05

Professional boundaries in fitness establish clear limits and expectations in trainer-client relationships to maintain ethical practice, prevent exploitation, and ensure effective service delivery. These boundaries protect both parties while fostering a safe, respectful environment conducive to achieving fitness goals.

**Core Components:**
- **Role Definition:** Clear establishment of professional roles versus personal relationships, maintaining appropriate trainer-client dynamics and avoiding dual relationships ([[P1. Ethical Training and Coaching]], [[P4. Evidence-Based Coaching Methodologies]], [[F4. Behavioral Psychology in Fitness]])
- **Physical Boundaries:** Appropriate physical contact protocols, spotting techniques, and body positioning during exercise instruction while respecting personal space and cultural considerations ([[A18. Proper Lifting Mechanics]], [[P8. Cultural Competency in Fitness]], [[P5. Informed Consent in Fitness]])
- **Communication Standards:** Professional language use, appropriate topics of conversation, and maintaining focus on fitness-related goals rather than personal issues ([[P7. Confidentiality and Privacy Protection]], [[D2. Diet Adherence and Behavior Change]], [[N3. Building Sustainable Fitness Habits]])
- **Scope of Practice:** Understanding and adhering to professional qualifications, avoiding services outside expertise areas, and making appropriate referrals to other healthcare professionals ([[G3. Training with Health Limitations & Chronic Conditions]], [[IX-5. Introduction to Rehabilitation Principles]], [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]])
- **Power Dynamics:** Recognition of inherent power imbalances in trainer-client relationships and strategies to minimize exploitation or manipulation risks ([[P3. Body Image and Fitness Culture]], [[I2. Female-Specific Physiological Considerations]], [[P10. Inclusive Fitness Environment Design]])
