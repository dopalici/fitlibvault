### Geriatric Exercise Protocols
**ID:** G6 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Geriatric exercise protocols focus on maintaining and improving functional capacity, independence, and quality of life in older adults through evidence-based exercise interventions. These protocols address age-related physiological changes including sarcopenia, bone loss, and cardiovascular decline while emphasizing fall prevention and cognitive health benefits.

**Core Components:**
- **Functional Exercise Focus:** Prioritizing movements that support activities of daily living and independence, connecting to [[L1. Movement Patterns and Functional Training]] and [[N1. Fitness Across the Lifespan]]
- **Sarcopenia Prevention:** Resistance training protocols specifically designed to combat age-related muscle loss, linking to [[B4. Strength Training Principles]] and [[A43. Muscular Adaptations to Training]]
- **Balance and Fall Prevention:** Targeted interventions to improve postural stability and reduce fall risk, relating to [[L3. Balance and Coordination Training]] and [[IX-2. Injury Prevention Strategies]]
- **Cardiovascular Health Maintenance:** Age-appropriate aerobic exercise protocols that support heart health without excessive stress, connecting to [[E2. Steady-State Cardio (LISS - Low-Intensity Steady State)]] and [[A39. Cardiovascular Adaptations to Training]]
- **Cognitive and Social Benefits:** Understanding exercise's role in cognitive preservation and social engagement in older populations, linking to [[A65. Exercise-Induced Neuroplasticity]] and [[F4. Behavioral Psychology in Fitness]]
