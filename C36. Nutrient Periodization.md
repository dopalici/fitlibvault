### C36. Nutrient Periodization

**ID:** C36-NUTRIENT-PERIODIZATION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Nutrient periodization involves strategically manipulating macronutrient and micronutrient intake to align with training phases, performance goals, and physiological adaptations. This approach optimizes nutrient timing to support energy demands, recovery, and adaptation while minimizing metabolic stress and maximizing training outcomes.

**Core Components:**

- **Macronutrient Cycling:** Systematic variation of carbohydrate, protein, and fat intake based on training intensity, volume, and phase objectives ([[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]], [[C17. Carb Cycling]])
- **Training Phase Alignment:** Matching nutritional strategies to preparatory, competitive, and recovery phases of periodized training programs ([[A25. Principle of Periodization]], [[K2. Advanced Periodization Strategies]])
- **Metabolic Adaptation Support:** Using targeted nutrition to enhance specific energy system development and metabolic flexibility ([[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]], [[A35. Metabolic Adaptations to Training]])
- **Recovery Optimization:** Implementing phase-specific recovery nutrition protocols to support adaptation and prevent overreaching ([[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]], [[M2. Overtraining and Functional Overreaching]])
- **Performance Peaking:** Strategic nutritional loading and tapering approaches to optimize competition readiness ([[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]], [[C28. Nutrition for Strength Power Athletes]])
