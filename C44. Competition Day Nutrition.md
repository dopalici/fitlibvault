### C44. Competition Day Nutrition

**ID:** C44-COMPETITION-DAY-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Competition day nutrition involves implementing precise nutritional strategies to optimize performance, maintain energy availability, and support physiological function during competitive events. This approach requires careful planning of pre-competition loading, during-event fueling, and post-competition recovery to maximize athletic performance while minimizing gastrointestinal distress and metabolic disruption.

**Core Components:**

- **Pre-Competition Strategies:** Optimizing glycogen stores, hydration status, and nutrient timing in the hours and days leading up to competition ([[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]], [[C4. Hydration]])
- **During-Event Fueling:** Implementing evidence-based protocols for maintaining blood glucose, electrolyte balance, and hydration during competition ([[C5. Pre, During, and Post-Exercise Nutrition]], [[C5. Electrolytes]])
- **Sport-Specific Applications:** Tailoring nutrition strategies to the unique demands of different sports, event durations, and environmental conditions ([[X. Sports-Specific Training]], [[C47. Heat Stress Nutrition]])
- **Gastrointestinal Management:** Preventing and managing digestive issues through strategic food choices, timing, and gut training protocols ([[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]], [[C49. Multi-Day Event Nutrition]])
- **Recovery Initiation:** Beginning immediate post-competition nutrition strategies to support recovery and prepare for subsequent events or training ([[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]], [[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]])
