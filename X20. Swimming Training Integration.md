### X20. Swimming Training Integration
**ID:** X20-SWIMMING-INTEGRATION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Swimming training integration combines pool-based technique work with dry-land strength and conditioning to optimize stroke mechanics, power development, and race-specific conditioning. Training emphasizes stroke-specific movement patterns, shoulder health, and integration of aerobic and anaerobic training systems.

**Core Components:**
- **Stroke-Specific Dry-Land Training:** Land-based exercises that mimic swimming movements ([[X3.1. Dry-Land Training for Swimmers]]) including cable exercises, medicine ball work, and resistance band training to improve stroke power and technique transfer.
- **Shoulder Health and Injury Prevention:** Comprehensive rotator cuff strengthening, scapular stability work, and postural exercises to address the high-volume overhead movement demands of swimming training ([[IX-2. Injury Prevention Strategies]]).
- **Core Stability for Streamline Position:** Anti-extension exercises ([[EXL-84 Plank (Standard & Variations)]), rotational stability training ([[L4. Core Stability Training]]), and breathing pattern work to maintain optimal body position and reduce drag during swimming.
- **Power Development for Starts and Turns:** Plyometric training focusing on explosive push-offs ([[X12. Vertical Jump Training]]), reaction time improvement, and underwater dolphin kick strength using core and leg power exercises ([[EXL-01 Barbell Back Squat (High Bar & Low Bar)]]).
- **Event-Specific Energy System Training:** Integration of aerobic base building ([[E11. VO2 Max Training]]) for distance events with lactate tolerance training ([[E3. High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)]]) for sprint events, coordinated with technical swimming training ([[X3. Swimming]]).
