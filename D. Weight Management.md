# Section D: Weight Management

This section organizes the practical and evidence-based foundations of body-mass
change. Start here when you need a structured path for fat loss, appetite
management, and long-term maintenance instead of short-lived dieting cycles.
The goal is to connect physiology, behavior, and habit design so progress is
measurable, sustainable, and safe across training phases.
