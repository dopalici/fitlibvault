### X13. Baseball/Softball Training
**ID:** X13-BASEBALL-SOFTBALL **Created:** 2025-06-05

Baseball and softball training requires integration of explosive power development, rotational movement patterns, hand-eye coordination, and sport-specific conditioning to optimize performance in batting, throwing, fielding, and base running. Training emphasizes asymmetrical movement patterns and injury prevention for overhead throwing athletes.

**Core Components:**
- **Rotational Power Development:** Emphasis on medicine ball throws, cable woodchops ([[EXL-76 Cable Woodchop (High-to-Low & Low-to-High)]]), and rotational resistance exercises to develop the kinetic chain for batting and throwing power ([[A31. Force-Velocity Profiling]]).
- **Throwing Mechanics and Arm Care:** Progressive throwing programs, shoulder stability exercises ([[L4. Core Stability Training]]), and scapular strengthening to prevent overuse injuries common in overhead athletes ([[IX-2. Injury Prevention Strategies]]).
- **Explosive Lower Body Power:** Plyometric training ([[X11.5 Plyometric Training for Sprinters]]), Olympic lift variations ([[B15. Olympic Weightlifting]]), and lateral movement drills to develop first-step quickness and jumping ability for fielding.
- **Hand-Eye Coordination and Reaction Training:** Visual tracking drills, reaction ball exercises, and sport-specific movement patterns to improve batting timing and fielding reactions ([[A33. Motor Learning and Skill Acquisition]]).
- **Asymmetrical Training Considerations:** Address imbalances from repetitive throwing and batting motions through unilateral exercises ([[B8. Free Weights (Barbells, Dumbbells)]]) and corrective movement patterns ([[A17. Movement Screening]]).
