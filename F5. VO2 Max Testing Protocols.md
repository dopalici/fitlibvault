### F5. VO2 Max Testing Protocols
**ID:** F5 **Created:** 2025-06-05

VO2 max testing protocols are standardized procedures designed to measure an individual's maximum oxygen uptake capacity, representing the gold standard for assessing aerobic fitness and cardiovascular health. These protocols provide crucial baseline data for training prescription, performance prediction, and monitoring adaptations to endurance training programs.

**Core Components:**
- **Graded Exercise Testing:** Progressive intensity protocols using treadmill or cycle ergometer with metabolic analysis equipment to measure oxygen consumption at exhaustion [[E11. VO2 Max Training]]
- **Protocol Selection:** Choice between continuous ramping, step protocols, or sport-specific testing modes based on athlete population and testing objectives [[A8. Cardiovascular Physiology Fundamentals]]
- **Equipment Calibration:** Proper setup and calibration of metabolic carts, gas analyzers, and ergometry equipment to ensure accurate measurement of respiratory gases [[J1. Wearable Technology and Fitness Tracking]]
- **Safety Monitoring:** Real-time assessment of heart rate, blood pressure, ECG, and subjective exertion scales with established termination criteria [[IX-6. Exercise Contraindications]]
- **Data Interpretation:** Analysis of VO2 plateaus, respiratory exchange ratios, ventilatory thresholds, and normative comparisons for population-specific assessment [[E12. Lactate Threshold Training]]
