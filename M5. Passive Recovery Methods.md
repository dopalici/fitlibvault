### M5. Passive Recovery Methods
**ID:** M5-PASSIVE-RECOVERY **Created:** 2025-06-05

Passive recovery methods encompass techniques that require minimal to no physical activity, focusing on creating optimal conditions for physiological restoration and adaptation. These methods complement active recovery by providing complete rest from movement stress while potentially enhancing recovery through environmental manipulation, manual therapy, and targeted interventions.

**Core Components:**
- **Environmental Recovery Modalities:** Cold water immersion, heat therapy, and contrast therapy protocols that manipulate temperature to influence circulation and inflammation ([[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]], [[A27. Thermoregulation During Exercise]])
- **Manual Therapy Interventions:** Massage therapy, soft tissue mobilization, and bodywork techniques that promote blood flow and reduce perceived muscle tension ([[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]], [[L2. Mobility and Flexibility Development]])
- **Compression and Elevation:** Pneumatic compression devices, compression garments, and positioning strategies to enhance venous return and reduce swelling ([[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]], [[A39. Cardiovascular Adaptations to Training]])
- **Complete Rest Protocols:** Strategic periods of total inactivity to allow cellular repair and energy substrate replenishment ([[A26. Principle of Recovery]], [[F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]])
- **Sensory Recovery Techniques:** Flotation tanks, meditation chambers, and environments designed to minimize sensory input and promote deep relaxation ([[M3. Stress Management and Performance]], [[F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]])
