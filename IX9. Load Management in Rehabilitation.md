### IX9. Load Management in Rehabilitation
**ID:** IX9-LOAD-MANAGEMENT-REHAB **Created:** 2025-06-05

Load management in rehabilitation involves the systematic application and progression of mechanical stress to promote optimal tissue adaptation while avoiding overload that could delay healing or cause re-injury. This approach balances the need for sufficient stimulus to drive adaptation with appropriate recovery to prevent tissue breakdown.

**Core Components:**
- **Tissue-Specific Loading Parameters:** Understanding load tolerance and adaptation timelines for different tissues using [[A29. Tissue Adaptation to Exercise (Molecular & Cellular)]] and [[A42. Connective Tissue Adaptations to Training]] principles
- **Progressive Overload Application:** Systematic increase in load magnitude, frequency, and complexity following [[B1. Progressive Overload]] and [[A19. Principle of Overload]] frameworks
- **Load Monitoring and Assessment:** Objective tracking of external and internal load responses using [[A48. Training Load Monitoring]] and [[A49. Recovery Monitoring and Assessment]] methodologies
- **Recovery Integration:** Balancing loading with adequate recovery periods following [[A26. Principle of Recovery]] and [[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]] guidelines
- **Individual Response Adaptation:** Modifying load progression based on individual healing rates and [[A23. Principle of Individuality]] considerations
