### C. Nutrition & Diet

## Quick Takeaway
Evidence-based nutrition principles from basic energy balance to advanced performance strategies, organized as a comprehensive reference for optimizing health, body composition, and athletic performance.

- Timing and quality enhance results: [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] and [[C30. Food Quality & Health (Whole vs Processed)]] optimize nutrient utilization

## Deep Dive

This nutrition section provides a systematic, evidence-based approach to dietary optimization. The content follows a hierarchical structure, progressing from fundamental principles to specialized applications.

### Foundational Energy Principles
All nutritional strategies build upon energy balance, detailed in [[C7. Energy Balance & Nutritional Aspects of Weight Management]]. Understanding how the body adapts through [[C8. Metabolic Adaptation (Adaptive Thermogenesis)]] informs realistic expectations and sustainable approaches.

Hydration and electrolyte management, covered in [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]], form critical but often overlooked foundations that significantly impact performance, recovery, and overall health outcomes.

### Strategic Macronutrient Management
Beyond basic calorie counting, optimal results require strategic macronutrient distribution through [[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]] and timing via [[C3. Nutrient Timing]]. These strategies maximize nutrient utilization and support specific training adaptations.

The role of [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]] extends beyond basic health maintenance to support optimal metabolic function and recovery processes.

## Practical Applications
- Start with energy balance: use [[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]] to establish appropriate energy intake
- Master label reading: implement [[C31. Reading Nutrition Labels]] for informed food selection
- Choose sustainable approaches: apply [[C18. Flexible Dieting (IIFYM - If It Fits Your Macros)]] principles for long-term adherence
- Develop healthy relationships: incorporate [[C19. Intuitive Eating]] concepts for psychological well-being
- Optimize recovery: implement [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]] strategies to support training adaptations
- Prioritize hydration: maintain proper [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]] status daily

## Common Mistakes
⚠️ Focusing on supplements before mastering basic nutrition fundamentals like energy balance and macronutrient distribution
⚠️ Choosing extreme dietary approaches that aren't sustainable long-term or compatible with lifestyle
⚠️ Ignoring individual responses and trying to force incompatible dietary approaches
⚠️ Neglecting basic [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]] in favor of more complex nutritional strategies
⚠️ Overlooking the importance of [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]] for overall health and nutrient absorption
⚠️ Expecting immediate results from dietary changes that require weeks to months for full adaptation
