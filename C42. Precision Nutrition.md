### C42. Precision Nutrition

**ID:** C42-PRECISION-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Precision nutrition involves personalizing dietary recommendations based on individual genetic, metabolic, microbiome, and lifestyle factors to optimize health and performance outcomes. This approach integrates advanced testing methodologies and data analysis to develop targeted nutritional strategies that account for individual variations in nutrient metabolism, absorption, and response patterns.

**Core Components:**

- **Genetic-Based Nutrition:** Utilizing genetic testing to understand individual variations in nutrient metabolism, food sensitivities, and optimal macronutrient ratios ([[K1. Genetic Factors in Athletic Performance]], [[A23. Principle of Individuality]])
- **Metabolic Phenotyping:** Assessing individual metabolic responses, insulin sensitivity, and substrate utilization patterns to guide nutritional strategies ([[A35. Metabolic Adaptations to Training]], [[C8. Metabolic Adaptation (Adaptive Thermogenesis)]])
- **Microbiome Analysis:** Incorporating gut microbiome composition and function into personalized nutrition recommendations ([[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]], [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]])
- **Biomarker Integration:** Using blood, saliva, and other biomarkers to monitor nutritional status and optimize supplement protocols ([[Q4. Interpreting Biometric & Progress Data]], [[C38. Micronutrient Timing]])
- **Technology Applications:** Leveraging wearable devices, continuous glucose monitors, and AI-driven platforms for real-time nutrition optimization ([[J1. Wearable Technology and Fitness Tracking]], [[Q8. Advanced Data Analysis for Self-Quantification]])
