# Section G: Special Populations

This section adapts core training principles for people whose needs differ from
general population templates. It focuses on safety constraints, progression
rate, medical context, and realistic adherence strategies. Use this section
when program quality depends on precision: pregnancy, age transitions, or
chronic-condition management where blanket recommendations often fail.
