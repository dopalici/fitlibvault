## N. Long-Term Development

This section centers on durability: how to train in ways that remain effective
for decades rather than single cycles. It links lifespan physiology, behavior
formation, and progression pacing so users can keep improving while reducing
dropout risk. Use it for strategic planning when consistency and longevity are
more important than short bursts of aggressive progress.
