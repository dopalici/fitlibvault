## I. Hormones and Fitness

This section explains how endocrine signals shape adaptation, recovery, and
performance outcomes. It is designed for practical decision-making: what to
monitor, when to suspect low-energy availability, and how physiology differs
across populations and life stages. Use these notes to connect biomarker trends
to program design without turning normal fluctuations into false alarms.
