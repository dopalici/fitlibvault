### IX13. Team-Based Injury Management
**ID:** IX13-TEAM-BASED-INJURY-MANAGEMENT **Created:** 2025-06-05

Team-based injury management involves coordinated care delivery through multidisciplinary healthcare teams working collaboratively to optimize injury prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation outcomes. This approach ensures comprehensive care by leveraging the expertise of various professionals while maintaining clear communication and shared decision-making.

**Core Components:**
- **Multidisciplinary Team Structure:** Coordination between physicians, physical therapists, athletic trainers, strength coaches, and sports psychologists following [[../ethics/P1. Ethical Training and Coaching]] and scope of practice guidelines
- **Communication Protocols:** Establishing clear channels for information sharing, progress updates, and collaborative decision-making among team members and with athletes
- **Role Definition and Coordination:** Clarifying responsibilities and expertise areas while ensuring seamless transitions between care phases from acute treatment through [[../health_conditions/IX-5. Introduction to Rehabilitation Principles]]
- **Integrated Treatment Planning:** Developing comprehensive care plans that incorporate medical treatment, rehabilitation, performance training, and psychological support using evidence-based approaches
- **Outcome Monitoring and Evaluation:** Collaborative assessment of treatment effectiveness and adjustment of interventions based on [[../integrated_approaches/O2. Measuring Fitness Progress]] and shared outcome metrics
