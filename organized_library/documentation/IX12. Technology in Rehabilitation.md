### IX12. Technology in Rehabilitation
**ID:** IX12-TECHNOLOGY-REHABILITATION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Technology in rehabilitation encompasses various digital tools, devices, and systems that enhance assessment, treatment, and monitoring of injury recovery. These technologies provide objective data, improve treatment precision, and enable remote monitoring to optimize rehabilitation outcomes and patient engagement.

**Core Components:**
- **Wearable Monitoring Systems:** Utilizing fitness trackers and sensors for real-time monitoring of activity, movement quality, and physiological responses following [[../technology/J1. Wearable Technology and Fitness Tracking]] and [[../equipment_technology/VIII-3. Wearable Technology (Fitness Trackers, Smartwatches, HRMs)]] protocols
- **Movement Analysis Technology:** Advanced biomechanical assessment tools including motion capture and force analysis systems using [[../foundation/A31. Force-Velocity Profiling]] and [[G-VII-2. Kinesiology Movement Analysis]] methodologies
- **Digital Rehabilitation Platforms:** App-based exercise prescription and progress tracking systems incorporating [[../equipment_technology/VIII-4. Fitness Apps]] and [[../integrated_approaches/O2. Measuring Fitness Progress]] capabilities
- **Telehealth and Remote Monitoring:** Virtual consultation and supervision platforms enabling continuous care and [[../foundation/A49. Recovery Monitoring and Assessment]] from distance
- **Biofeedback and Virtual Reality:** Real-time feedback systems for movement correction and pain management using immersive technologies and [[../foundation/A33. Motor Learning and Skill Acquisition]] principles
