### IX14. Injury Prevention Screening
**ID:** IX14-INJURY-PREVENTION-SCREENING **Created:** 2025-06-05

Injury prevention screening involves systematic assessment of risk factors, movement patterns, and physical characteristics to identify individuals at elevated injury risk and implement targeted interventions. This proactive approach aims to reduce injury incidence through early identification and correction of modifiable risk factors.

**Core Components:**
- **Movement Quality Assessment:** Comprehensive evaluation of fundamental movement patterns using [[../foundation/A17. Movement Screening]] and [[../foundation/A28. Advanced Movement Assessment Methodologies]] to identify dysfunctional patterns
- **Physical Performance Testing:** Assessment of strength, power, flexibility, and balance capabilities using [[../foundation/A32. Power Profiling and Testing]] and [[../cross_training/L3. Balance and Coordination Training]] protocols
- **Risk Factor Identification:** Evaluation of intrinsic and extrinsic factors including training history, previous injuries, biomechanical limitations, and psychosocial factors following [[../health_conditions/IX-2. Injury Prevention Strategies]]
- **Sport-Specific Screening Protocols:** Tailored assessments based on sport demands and common injury patterns for activities like [[../sports_specific/X1. Running (Track, Road, Trail)]] or [[../sports_specific/X4. Team Sports - General Considerations]]
- **Intervention Strategy Development:** Creating targeted programs to address identified deficits through corrective exercise, [[../cross_training/L2. Mobility and Flexibility Development]], and [[../cross_training/L4. Core Stability Training]]
