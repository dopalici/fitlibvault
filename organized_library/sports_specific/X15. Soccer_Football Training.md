### X15. Soccer/Football Training
**ID:** X15-SOCCER-FOOTBALL **Created:** 2025-06-05

Soccer/football training requires exceptional aerobic capacity combined with repeated sprint ability, ball skills, and multidirectional movement patterns. Training emphasizes endurance development while maintaining explosive power for sprinting, jumping, and change of direction during continuous play.

**Core Components:**
- **Aerobic Base Development:** High-volume aerobic training ([[../cardiovascular_training/E2. Steady-State Cardio (LISS - Low-Intensity Steady State)]]) combined with tempo runs to build the aerobic foundation required for 90-minute match play ([[../cardiovascular_training/E11. VO2 Max Training]]).
- **Repeated Sprint Ability:** Interval training that mimics soccer's intermittent high-intensity efforts with incomplete recovery ([[../cardiovascular_training/E3. High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)]]) to improve lactate clearance and sprint repeatability ([[../foundation/A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]]).
- **Multidirectional Movement and Agility:** Cone drills, ladder work, and sport-specific cutting patterns to improve change of direction speed and reduce injury risk ([[../cross_training/L3. Balance and Coordination Training]]).
- **Lower Body Power and Strength:** Emphasis on unilateral leg strength ([[EXL-36 Dumbbell Lunge (Static/Alternating/Walking)]]) and plyometric training for jumping, kicking power, and sprint acceleration ([[X11.5 Plyometric Training for Sprinters]]).
- **Core Stability for Ball Control:** Anti-extension and anti-rotation exercises ([[../cross_training/L4. Core Stability Training]]) to maintain posture and control during ball manipulation, tackling, and heading ([[../exercise_library/EXL-84 Plank (Standard & Variations)]]).
