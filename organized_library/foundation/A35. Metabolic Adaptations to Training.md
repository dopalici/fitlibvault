# A35. Ground Reaction Force Analysis

## Quick Takeaway

Ground reaction force analysis involves understanding the forces exerted between the body and the ground during movement, including assessment of force magnitude, direction, timing, and their relationship to performance outcomes.

## Key Points

- Force analysis accuracy improves by 20-30% with targeted assessment
- Force application efficiency increases by 15-25% with proper optimization
- Movement quality enhances by 25-35% with optimal force patterns
- Performance outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic analysis
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate force management
