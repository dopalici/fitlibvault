### C41. Adaptogens in Sports

**ID:** C41-ADAPTOGENS-SPORTS **Created:** 2025-06-05

Adaptogens are natural compounds that may help the body adapt to physical and psychological stressors while supporting homeostasis and recovery. In sports contexts, certain adaptogens show emerging evidence for stress management, fatigue reduction, and performance enhancement, though their classification often falls within investigational supplement categories requiring careful evaluation.

- **Stress Response Modulation:** Understanding how adaptogenic compounds may influence the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis and stress hormone responses to training ([[../foundation/A10. Hormonal Responses to Exercise]], [[../recovery/M3. Stress Management and Performance]])
- **Recovery Enhancement:** Evaluating evidence for adaptogenic support of recovery processes, sleep quality, and training adaptation ([[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]], [[../performance_factors/F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]])
- **Performance Applications:** Assessing research on adaptogenic effects on endurance, strength, and cognitive performance in athletic populations ([[C22. Supplements with LimitedMixed Evidence (Group B)]], [[../performance_factors/F2. Performance Optimization Factors]])
- **Safety and Quality:** Understanding the safety profiles, potential interactions, and quality control considerations for commonly used adaptogens ([[C23. Supplement Safety, Regulation, and Quality Control]], [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]])
- **Evidence-Based Selection:** Critically evaluating research quality and practical applications for specific adaptogenic compounds in sports nutrition protocols ([[../technology/J2. Evaluating Scientific Research in Fitness]], [[C20. Supplements (Overview & Framework)]])
