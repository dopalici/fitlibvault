### C40. Nootropics for Athletes

Nootropics are compounds that may enhance cognitive function, focus, and mental performance in athletes, potentially improving decision-making, reaction time, and training quality. While some nootropics have emerging evidence for cognitive enhancement, their application in sports requires careful evaluation of efficacy, safety, and anti-doping compliance considerations.
