### Competition Nutrition Protocols
**ID:** C86
**Created:** 2025-06-05 (Updated: 2025-01-27)

## Quick Takeaway
Competition nutrition protocols are systematic, sport-specific nutritional strategies that optimize performance through strategic pre-event preparation, during-event fueling, and immediate post-event recovery.

## Key Points
- Competition nutrition requires individualized protocols based on event duration, intensity, environmental conditions, and athlete tolerance
- Pre-competition preparation focuses on maximizing glycogen stores while minimizing gastrointestinal distress through strategic timing
- During-event fueling optimizes carbohydrate and fluid delivery to maintain blood glucose and prevent dehydration
- Post-competition nutrition immediately targets glycogen replenishment and protein synthesis for rapid recovery
- Event-specific protocols differ significantly between endurance, strength/power, and team sport competitions

## Deep Dive

### Pre-Competition Preparation
Pre-competition nutrition begins 1-7 days before competition, depending on event demands. The primary goals are maximizing muscle and liver glycogen stores while ensuring gastrointestinal comfort and optimal hydration status.

Strategic [[C17. Carb Cycling]] and [[C44. Competition Day Nutrition]] protocols help athletes achieve peak glycogen storage. For endurance events lasting >90 minutes, classical or modified carbohydrate loading protocols can increase muscle glycogen stores by 20-40% above normal levels.

Meal timing becomes critical in the final 24-48 hours, with athletes practicing their exact pre-competition meal schedule during training to identify optimal timing and food choices that minimize GI distress while maintaining energy availability.

### During-Event Fueling
During-event nutrition strategies vary dramatically based on exercise duration and intensity. Events lasting <60 minutes typically require only fluid replacement, while longer events benefit from systematic carbohydrate and electrolyte delivery.

Optimal [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]] replacement prevents performance-limiting dehydration and maintains electrolyte balance. Current recommendations suggest 30-60g carbohydrates per hour for events 1-2.5 hours, increasing to 60-90g/hour for ultra-endurance events when multiple carbohydrate sources are used.

The timing and delivery method (liquid vs. solid) must be individualized based on sport demands, environmental conditions, and individual tolerance patterns established during training.

### Post-Competition Recovery
Immediate post-competition nutrition focuses on rapid glycogen replenishment and protein synthesis initiation. The "golden window" concept emphasizes consuming carbohydrates and protein within 30-60 minutes post-exercise when muscle uptake rates are maximized.

Implementing [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]] principles immediately post-competition can significantly accelerate recovery timeline and prepare athletes for subsequent training sessions or competition rounds.

### Sport-Specific Protocols
Different competitive formats require distinct nutritional approaches. Endurance athletes benefit from [[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]] strategies, while [[C28. Nutrition for Strength Power Athletes]] focuses on maintaining power output and supporting rapid energy system recovery between efforts.

Team sport athletes require hybrid approaches that support both aerobic and anaerobic energy systems while accommodating intermittent high-intensity efforts throughout competition.

- Study type: Position statement/systematic review
- Evidence quality: High (comprehensive evidence synthesis)

## Practical Applications

**Pre-Competition Timeline:**
- 3-7 days before: Begin carbohydrate loading if event >90 minutes
- 24-48 hours before: Practice exact competition day meal timing
- 3-4 hours before: Consume 1-4g carbohydrate/kg body weight
- 1 hour before: Light carbohydrate snack (0.5-1g/kg) if tolerated

**During-Event Guidelines:**
- <60 minutes: Water only, mouth rinse with carbohydrate solution
- 1-2.5 hours: 30-60g carbohydrate/hour + 150-250ml fluid every 15-20 minutes
- >2.5 hours: 60-90g carbohydrate/hour using multiple sources

**Post-Competition Protocol:**
- Within 30 minutes: 1-1.2g carbohydrate/kg + 20-25g high-quality protein
- Within 2 hours: Complete meal with 3:1 or 4:1 carbohydrate:protein ratio

## Common Mistakes
⚠️ Trying new foods or supplements on competition day without prior testing during training
⚠️ Over-consuming fluids leading to hyponatremia, especially in ultra-endurance events
⚠️ Ignoring individual GI tolerance and following generic recommendations
⚠️ Focusing only on during-event nutrition while neglecting pre-competition preparation
⚠️ Failing to account for environmental conditions (heat, altitude) in hydration planning
