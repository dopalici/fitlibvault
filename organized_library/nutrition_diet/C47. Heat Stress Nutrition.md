### C47. Heat Stress Nutrition

**ID:** C47-HEAT-STRESS-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Heat stress nutrition involves specialized nutritional strategies to support thermoregulation, maintain performance, and prevent heat-related illness during exercise in hot environments. This approach addresses increased fluid and electrolyte losses, altered metabolic demands, and enhanced oxidative stress associated with elevated core temperatures and prolonged heat exposure.

**Core Components:**

- **Thermoregulatory Support:** Implementing nutritional strategies to enhance heat dissipation, maintain core temperature regulation, and support cardiovascular function ([[../foundation/A27. Thermoregulation During Exercise]], [[../foundation/A39. Cardiovascular Adaptations to Training]])
- **Fluid and Electrolyte Management:** Developing precise hydration protocols to replace sweat losses and maintain plasma volume during heat stress ([[C4. Hydration]], [[C5. Electrolytes]])
- **Pre-Cooling Nutrition:** Using strategic nutritional interventions to lower core temperature before exercise in hot conditions ([[C5. Pre, During, and Post-Exercise Nutrition]], [[C3. Nutrient Timing]])
- **Oxidative Stress Management:** Implementing antioxidant strategies to combat increased free radical production associated with heat stress ([[C34. Antioxidants]], [[../foundation/A35. Metabolic Adaptations to Training]])
- **Heat Acclimatization Support:** Supporting physiological adaptations to repeated heat exposure through targeted nutrition protocols ([[../foundation/A36. Structural Adaptations to Training]], [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]])
