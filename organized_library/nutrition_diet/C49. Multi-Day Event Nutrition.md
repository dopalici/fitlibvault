### C49. Multi-Day Event Nutrition

**ID:** C49-MULTI-DAY-EVENT-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Multi-day event nutrition involves strategic nutritional planning for competitions or training camps spanning multiple consecutive days, requiring sustained energy availability, accelerated recovery, and prevention of cumulative fatigue. This specialized approach addresses the unique challenges of maintaining performance standards while managing glycogen depletion, immune suppression, and gastrointestinal stress across extended periods.

**Core Components:**

- **Glycogen Replenishment:** Implementing rapid and effective carbohydrate loading strategies between training sessions or competitive events ([[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]], [[../foundation/A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]])
- **Recovery Acceleration:** Using evidence-based nutrition protocols to speed recovery processes and minimize performance degradation across multiple days ([[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]], [[../recovery/M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]])
- **Cumulative Stress Management:** Addressing the progressive accumulation of physiological stress through targeted anti-inflammatory and antioxidant strategies ([[C33. Anti-Inflammatory FoodsDiets]], [[C34. Antioxidants]])
- **Gastrointestinal Tolerance:** Maintaining digestive function and nutrient absorption despite repeated feeding challenges and stress-induced GI dysfunction ([[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]], [[C44. Competition Day Nutrition]])
- **Immune Support:** Preventing illness and supporting immune function during periods of intensified training or competition ([[../foundation/A38. Immune System Adaptations to Training]], [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]])
