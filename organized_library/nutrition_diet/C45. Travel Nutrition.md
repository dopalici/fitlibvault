### C45. Travel Nutrition

**ID:** C45-TRAVEL-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Travel nutrition addresses the unique nutritional challenges athletes face when competing or training away from home, including time zone changes, altered meal schedules, and limited food access. This specialized approach focuses on maintaining performance standards while managing jet lag, digestive disruption, and dietary consistency across different environments and cultures.

**Core Components:**

- **Jet Lag Management:** Using strategic nutrition timing and specific nutrients to minimize circadian rhythm disruption and accelerate adaptation to new time zones ([[C43. Circadian Nutrition]], [[../performance_factors/F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]])
- **Food Safety and Selection:** Implementing protocols for safe food choices, hydration sources, and meal planning in unfamiliar environments ([[C30. Food Quality & Health (Whole vs Processed)]], [[C4. Hydration]])
- **Portable Nutrition Solutions:** Developing practical strategies for maintaining nutritional standards through travel-friendly foods and supplements ([[C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]], [[C5. Pre, During, and Post-Exercise Nutrition]])
- **Gastrointestinal Adaptation:** Managing digestive challenges from dietary changes, stress, and altered eating patterns during travel ([[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]], [[../recovery/M3. Stress Management and Performance]])
- **Competition Preparation:** Ensuring optimal nutritional status for competition despite travel-related disruptions to normal routines ([[C44. Competition Day Nutrition]], [[C37. Sports Nutrition Periodization]])
