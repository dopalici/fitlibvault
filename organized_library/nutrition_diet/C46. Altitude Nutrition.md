### C46. Altitude Nutrition

**ID:** C46-ALTITUDE-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Altitude nutrition addresses the specific nutritional adaptations and requirements for training or competing at elevation, where reduced oxygen availability creates unique physiological stresses. This specialized approach focuses on supporting acclimatization, maintaining performance, and managing altitude-related symptoms through targeted nutritional strategies and supplementation protocols.

**Core Components:**

- **Acclimatization Support:** Implementing nutritional strategies to facilitate physiological adaptation to altitude, including enhanced oxygen transport and metabolic efficiency ([[../foundation/A39. Cardiovascular Adaptations to Training]], [[../foundation/A40. Respiratory Adaptations to Training]])
- **Hydration Management:** Addressing increased fluid losses and dehydration risk at altitude through strategic hydration and electrolyte protocols ([[C4. Hydration]], [[C5. Electrolytes]])
- **Metabolic Adaptation:** Supporting altered energy metabolism and substrate utilization patterns that occur during altitude exposure ([[../foundation/A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]], [[../foundation/A35. Metabolic Adaptations to Training]])
- **Iron and Hematological Support:** Optimizing iron status and supporting increased red blood cell production during altitude adaptation ([[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]], [[../foundation/A64. Oxygen Sensing Pathways]])
- **Symptom Management:** Using evidence-based nutritional approaches to minimize altitude sickness symptoms and support recovery ([[C34. Antioxidants]], [[C41. Adaptogens in Sports]])
