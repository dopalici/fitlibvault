## Deep Dive

This nutrition section provides a systematic approach to understanding and implementing evidence-based dietary strategies. The content is organized hierarchically, moving from foundational concepts to specialized applications.

### Foundational Principles
The foundation begins with understanding energy balance through [[C7. Energy Balance & Nutritional Aspects of Weight Management]] and how the body adapts via [[C8. Metabolic Adaptation (Adaptive Thermogenesis)]]. These concepts inform all subsequent nutritional decisions.

Proper hydration and electrolyte balance form another cornerstone, covered in [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]]. These often-overlooked factors significantly impact performance and recovery.

### Macronutrient Strategy
Beyond basic calorie counting, optimal results require strategic macronutrient distribution and timing. [[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]] provides the framework, while [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] addresses when to consume specific nutrients for maximum benefit.

### Dietary Approaches
The section covers major dietary strategies, from traditional approaches like the [[C10. Mediterranean Diet]] to specialized protocols like [[C13. Ketogenic Diet]] and [[C17. Carb Cycling]]. Each approach is evaluated based on research evidence and practical considerations.

### Supplementation Framework
Supplements are organized by evidence quality, with [[C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]] covering those with strong research support, while [[C22. Supplements with LimitedMixed Evidence (Group B)]] addresses commonly used but less proven options.

### Goal-Specific Applications
Specialized sections address nutrition for specific outcomes, including detailed protocols for muscle gain, fat loss, and various performance demands through [[C35. Specialized Nutrition for Performance Demands]].

## Research Evidence
Each subsection contains peer-reviewed research citations supporting the recommendations. Evidence quality varies by topic, with stronger support for basic principles like energy balance and weaker evidence for some specialized approaches and supplements.

## Practical Applications
- Begin with [[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]] to establish energy needs
- Use [[C31. Reading Nutrition Labels]] to make informed food choices
- Implement [[C18. Flexible Dieting (IIFYM - If It Fits Your Macros)]] for sustainable adherence
- Consider [[C19. Intuitive Eating]] principles for long-term relationship with food
- Apply [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]] strategies to support training adaptations

## Common Mistakes
⚠️ Focusing on supplements before mastering basic nutrition fundamentals
⚠️ Choosing extreme dietary approaches that aren't sustainable long-term
⚠️ Ignoring individual responses and trying to force incompatible approaches
⚠️ Neglecting [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]] in favor of more complex strategies
⚠️ Missing the importance of [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]] for overall health
