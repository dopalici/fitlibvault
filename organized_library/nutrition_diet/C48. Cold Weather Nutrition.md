### C48. Cold Weather Nutrition

**ID:** C48-COLD-WEATHER-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Cold weather nutrition addresses the unique nutritional demands of training and competing in cold environments, where increased energy expenditure for thermogenesis and altered physiological responses require specialized dietary strategies. This approach focuses on maintaining core temperature, supporting immune function, and optimizing energy availability during cold exposure while managing dehydration risks and nutrient absorption changes.

**Core Components:**

- **Thermogenesis Support:** Providing adequate caloric intake and specific nutrients to support increased energy demands for heat production and temperature maintenance ([[../foundation/A1. Energy Balance]], [[../foundation/A27. Thermoregulation During Exercise]])
- **Cold-Induced Adaptations:** Supporting physiological adaptations to cold exposure including metabolic flexibility and substrate utilization changes ([[../foundation/A35. Metabolic Adaptations to Training]], [[../foundation/A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]])
- **Immune Function Maintenance:** Implementing nutritional strategies to support immune system function during cold stress and prevent illness ([[../foundation/A38. Immune System Adaptations to Training]], [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]])
- **Hydration in Cold Conditions:** Managing dehydration risks that are often underestimated in cold environments and addressing altered thirst mechanisms ([[C4. Hydration]], [[C5. Electrolytes]])
- **Performance Optimization:** Maintaining power output, endurance capacity, and cognitive function during cold weather exercise ([[C28. Nutrition for Strength Power Athletes]], [[C40. Nootropics for Athletes]])
