### C37. Sports Nutrition Periodization

**ID:** C37-SPORTS-NUTRITION-PERIODIZATION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Sports nutrition periodization systematically aligns nutritional strategies with athletic training cycles to optimize performance, adaptation, and recovery across different phases of competition preparation. This evidence-based approach integrates sport-specific energy demands with periodized training loads to maximize competitive outcomes while maintaining athlete health and metabolic flexibility.

**Core Components:**

- **Competition Phase Nutrition:** Tailoring macronutrient ratios and timing to support peak performance demands during competitive periods ([[C44. Competition Day Nutrition]], [[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]])
- **Training Load Synchronization:** Matching caloric and macronutrient intake to varying training intensities and volumes across mesocycles ([[../foundation/A48. Training Load Monitoring]], [[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]])
- **Adaptation Phase Support:** Implementing specific nutritional protocols to enhance training adaptations during base, build, and peak phases ([[C25. Nutrition for Muscle Gain (Hypertrophy)]], [[../foundation/A36. Structural Adaptations to Training]])
- **Recovery Periodization:** Cycling recovery nutrition strategies to prevent overreaching and optimize adaptation between training blocks ([[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]], [[../recovery/M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]])
- **Sport-Specific Applications:** Customizing periodization approaches for endurance, strength, power, and team sport demands ([[../sports_specific/X. Sports-Specific Training]], [[C28. Nutrition for Strength Power Athletes]])
