### Recovery Testing Methods
**ID:** F18 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Recovery testing methods involve systematic assessment of physiological and psychological markers that indicate an athlete's readiness to train and compete effectively. These testing protocols enable coaches and athletes to optimize training loads and prevent overtraining through objective monitoring of recovery status.

**Core Components:**
- **Heart Rate Variability (HRV) Assessment:** Non-invasive measurement of autonomic nervous system recovery using [[../recovery/M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]] principles
- **Biochemical Marker Analysis:** Blood and saliva testing for stress hormones, inflammatory markers, and metabolic indicators related to [[../foundation/A37. Hormonal Adaptations to Training]]
- **Subjective Recovery Questionnaires:** Validated tools assessing perceived recovery, sleep quality, and psychological readiness incorporating [[F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]] factors
- **Performance-Based Recovery Tests:** Functional assessments using countermovement jumps, grip strength, and other neuromuscular markers to evaluate [[../foundation/A34. Neural Adaptations to Training]]
- **Sleep and Stress Monitoring:** Comprehensive assessment protocols examining sleep architecture and stress responses affecting [[../recovery/M3. Stress Management and Performance]]
