### F7. Anaerobic Power Testing
**ID:** F7 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Anaerobic power testing encompasses various protocols designed to assess an individual's capacity to generate maximum power output during brief, high-intensity efforts that primarily rely on phosphocreatine and glycolytic energy systems. These assessments are essential for athletes in power-dependent sports and provide valuable data for training program design and performance monitoring.

**Core Components:**
- **Peak Power Assessment:** Measurement of highest instantaneous power output during maximal efforts using cycle ergometers, force plates, or sport-specific equipment [[../foundation/A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]]
- **Mean Power Evaluation:** Analysis of sustained power output over specific durations (10-60 seconds) to assess anaerobic capacity and power endurance [[../resistance_training/B13. Strength Training Goals (Max Strength, Power, Hypertrophy, Endurance)]]
- **Fatigue Index Calculation:** Quantification of power decline during sustained maximal efforts to evaluate neuromuscular fatigue resistance and recovery capacity [[../foundation/A7. Muscle Physiology Fundamentals]]
- **Protocol Standardization:** Proper warm-up procedures, resistance settings, duration parameters, and recovery periods to ensure reliable and valid test results [[F2. Performance Optimization Factors]]
- **Sport-Specific Applications:** Adaptation of testing protocols to match the demands of specific sports and training goals through appropriate movement patterns and energy system emphasis [[../sports_specific/X4. Team Sports - General Considerations]]
