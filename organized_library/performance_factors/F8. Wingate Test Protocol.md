### F8. Wingate Test Protocol
**ID:** F8 **Created:** 2025-06-05

The Wingate Test Protocol is a standardized 30-second maximal anaerobic power test performed on a cycle ergometer that measures peak power output, mean power, and fatigue index. This protocol represents the gold standard for assessing anaerobic power and capacity, providing reliable data for athlete evaluation, training prescription, and research applications across various sports and populations.

**Core Components:**
- **Equipment Calibration:** Proper setup of mechanically-braked cycle ergometer with accurate resistance calculation based on body weight (typically 0.075 kg/kg body weight) and flywheel calibration [[../technology/J1. Wearable Technology and Fitness Tracking]]
- **Pre-Test Procedures:** Standardized warm-up protocol including 5-10 minutes moderate cycling with practice sprints, proper seat adjustment, and participant instruction for maximal effort [[F2. Performance Optimization Factors]]
- **Test Execution:** 30-second all-out effort with immediate resistance application, continuous encouragement, and measurement of pedal revolutions every 5-second interval [[../foundation/A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]]
- **Data Analysis:** Calculation of peak power (highest 5-second power output), mean power (average power over 30 seconds), and fatigue index (percentage decline from peak to minimum power) [[../foundation/A7. Muscle Physiology Fundamentals]]
- **Normative Comparisons:** Interpretation of results using population-specific norms and sport-specific standards to assess anaerobic performance relative to training status and competitive level [[../integrated_approaches/O2. Measuring Fitness Progress]]
