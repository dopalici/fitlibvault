### Testing Reliability and Validity

Testing reliability and validity represent fundamental psychometric properties that determine the quality and trustworthiness of performance assessments. Reliability refers to the consistency of test results across repeated measurements, while validity indicates whether a test actually measures what it claims to measure. Understanding and establishing these properties is essential for making confident decisions based on performance testing data and ensuring that assessment protocols meet scientific standards.

**Core Components:**
- **Reliability Assessment:** Evaluation of test-retest reliability, inter-rater reliability, and internal consistency for [[../foundation/A50. Performance Testing and Evaluation]] protocols
- **Validity Types:** Understanding construct, criterion, and content validity in the context of [[../foundation/A31. Force-Velocity Profiling]] and [[../foundation/A32. Power Profiling and Testing]] assessments
- **Measurement Error:** Quantification of systematic and random error sources that affect test outcomes and interpretation of [[../key_overviews/Q4. Interpreting Biometric & Progress Data]]
- **Standardization Protocols:** Development of consistent testing procedures to minimize variability and enhance reliability across different testing sessions
- **Quality Control:** Implementation of ongoing monitoring procedures to maintain test quality and detect measurement drift over time, aligned with [[../technology/J2. Evaluating Scientific Research in Fitness]] standards
