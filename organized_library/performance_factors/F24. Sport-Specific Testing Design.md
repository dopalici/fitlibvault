### Sport-Specific Testing Design

Sport-specific testing design involves creating customized assessment protocols that closely match the physiological demands, movement patterns, and performance requirements of particular sports or activities. These tailored testing approaches provide more relevant and actionable performance data compared to generic fitness assessments.

**Core Components:**
- **Movement Pattern Analysis:** Development of tests that replicate sport-specific movement patterns incorporating principles from [[../documentation/G-VII-2. Kinesiology Movement Analysis]] and sport demands
- **Energy System Specificity:** Testing protocols designed to match the metabolic demands of specific sports using [[../foundation/A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]] knowledge
- **Skill-Performance Integration:** Assessment methods that combine technical skill evaluation with physical performance measures relevant to sport success
- **Position-Specific Requirements:** Tailored testing for different positions within team sports reflecting [[../sports_specific/X4. Team Sports - General Considerations]] and individual sport demands
- **Seasonal Testing Periodization:** Strategic timing of assessments aligned with training phases and competition schedules incorporating [[../foundation/A25. Principle of Periodization]]
