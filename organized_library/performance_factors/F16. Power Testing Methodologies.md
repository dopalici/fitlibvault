### Power Testing Methodologies

Power testing methodologies encompass various assessment techniques for measuring an athlete's ability to generate force rapidly, including both upper and lower body power outputs. These methodologies provide critical insights into neuromuscular function and explosive performance capabilities essential for athletic success across multiple sports.

**Core Components:**
- **Vertical Jump Testing:** Comprehensive assessment protocols including countermovement jumps and squat jumps utilizing [[../sports_specific/X12. Vertical Jump Training]] principles
- **Force-Velocity Profiling:** Advanced testing methodologies to determine individual [[../foundation/A31. Force-Velocity Profiling]] characteristics and optimal training zones
- **Wingate Testing:** Standardized anaerobic power assessment protocols measuring peak and mean power output over specific durations
- **Olympic Lifting Power Assessment:** Power measurement during [[../resistance_training/B15. Olympic Weightlifting]] movements with velocity-based training technologies
- **Sport-Specific Power Tests:** Tailored assessments reflecting movement patterns from [[../sports_specific/X4. Team Sports - General Considerations]] and other athletic disciplines
