### **EXL-48: Seated Leg Curl (Machine)**
**Primary Movers (Agonists):** The hamstring complex drives the movement, including:
- Biceps Femoris (both short and long heads)
- Semitendinosus 
- Semimembranosus

**Secondary Movers (Synergists):** Supporting muscles include the gastrocnemius (assists knee flexion), popliteus, sartorius, and gracilis.

**Stabilizers:** Core musculature maintains proper positioning [[L4. Core Stability Training]], while hip flexors help maintain the seated posture throughout the movement.

### **EXL-48: Seated Leg Curl (Machine)**
Following [[A18. Proper Lifting Mechanics]] principles:

**Setup Phase:**
- Adjust machine settings so knee joint aligns with the machine's axis of rotation
- Position ankle pad comfortably just above ankles/shoes
- Sit with back flat against pad, gripping handles for stability
- Ensure full range of motion is available

**Movement Execution:**
- Exhale while flexing knees, curling ankle pad toward glutes
- Focus on contracting hamstrings throughout the movement
- Pause briefly at peak contraction (1-2 seconds)
- Inhale while slowly returning to starting position
- Maintain tension by avoiding weight stack contact between reps

### **EXL-48: Seated Leg Curl (Machine)**
Research demonstrates that seated leg curls effectively activate the hamstring muscles with high EMG activity levels (Onishi et al., 2002). Studies show that machine-based hamstring exercises can produce significant strength gains while minimizing injury risk compared to free-weight alternatives (Zebis et al., 2013). The seated position allows for consistent form and progressive overload application, key factors in muscle hypertrophy (Schoenfeld, 2010).

### **EXL-48: Seated Leg Curl (Machine)**
- **Beginner Protocol:** 3 sets of 12-15 reps at 60-70% estimated 1RM
- **Hypertrophy Focus:** 3-4 sets of 8-12 reps with 2-second pause at contraction
- **Strength Building:** 4-5 sets of 6-8 reps with heavier resistance
- **Injury Prevention:** 2-3 sets of 15-20 reps with controlled tempo
- **Frequency:** 2-3 times per week with 48-72 hours recovery between sessions

### **EXL-48: Seated Leg Curl (Machine)**
⚠️ **Hip Lifting:** Avoid lifting hips off the seat pad, which reduces hamstring isolation and engages hip flexors

⚠️ **Momentum Usage:** Don't swing or jerk the weight - maintain smooth, controlled movement throughout

⚠️ **Incomplete Range of Motion:** Ensure full knee flexion at the top and controlled extension without hyperextension

⚠️ **Weight Stack Contact:** Prevent the weight stack from touching between reps to maintain constant muscle tension

⚠️ **Poor Machine Setup:** Improper ankle pad or seat positioning reduces effectiveness and may cause discomfort
