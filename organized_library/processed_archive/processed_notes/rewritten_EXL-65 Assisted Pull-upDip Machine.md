### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**
Assisted pull-up/dip machines use counterweight to reduce effective bodyweight, making these challenging exercises accessible while building strength toward unassisted performance.

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**
- Provides adjustable assistance (more counterweight = easier exercise)
- Allows proper movement patterns while building strength progressively
- Essential bridge exercise for developing pull-up and dip strength
- Decrease assistance weight over time to progress toward bodyweight versions
- Maintain strict form even with assistance to maximize strength transfer

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**
The assisted pull-up/dip machine [[B10. Machine Training]] provides adjustable counterweight assistance to reduce the effective bodyweight lifted during pull-ups/chin-ups or dips. This makes these challenging bodyweight exercises accessible to individuals not yet strong enough to perform them unassisted while maintaining proper movement patterns.

**Difficulty Level:** Beginner to Intermediate (depending on assistance level)

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**

**Pull-up Assist:**
- **Primary Movers:** Latissimus Dorsi, Teres Major, Biceps Brachii (See [[EXL-81 Pull-up  Chin-up (Bodyweight)]] for more detail)

**Dip Assist:**
- **Primary Movers:** Pectoralis Major (lower), Triceps Brachii, Anterior Deltoid (See [[EXL-82 Dip (Parallel Bar)]] for more detail)

**Stabilizers (Both):** Core musculature [[L4. Core Stability Training]], Scapular stabilizers

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**

**Setup:**
- Select desired assistance weight (more weight = more assistance = easier)
- Place knees or feet securely on the assistance platform
- Grasp pull-up or dip handles with appropriate grip

**Execution (Pull-up Assist):**
Follow pull-up form cues [[EXL-81 Pull-up  Chin-up (Bodyweight)]]:
- Start from hang position
- Initiate with scapular depression/retraction
- Pull chest towards bar until chin clears
- Lower under control to full extension
- Machine assistance helps on the way up

**Execution (Dip Assist):**
Follow dip form cues [[EXL-82 Dip (Parallel Bar)]]:
- Start with arms extended
- Lower body by bending elbows (keeping them tucked)
- Descend until upper arms are near parallel to floor (or comfortable depth)
- Press back up to full extension
- Machine assistance helps on the way up

**Breathing:** Exhale during exertion (pulling up/pressing up), inhale during lowering/return

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**

**Progression Path:**
- Decrease assistance (reduce counterweight) [[B1. Progressive Overload]]
- Increase reps/sets [[B5. Training Volume and Frequency]]
- Tempo variations (slow eccentric [[B20. Eccentric Training]], pauses)
- Transition to band assisted pull-ups/dips [[B11. Resistance Bands]]
- Transition to negative pull-ups/dips (eccentric only)
- Progress to unassisted bodyweight pull-ups/chin-ups [[EXL-81 Pull-up  Chin-up (Bodyweight)]] or dips [[EXL-82 Dip (Parallel Bar)]]

**Regression Path:**
- Increase assistance (more counterweight)
- Lat pulldowns [[EXL-56 Lat Pulldown (Machine - Wide Grip)]] (for pull-up pattern)
- Triceps extensions [[EXL-59 Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)]] or triceps pushdowns [[EXL-67 Cable Triceps Pushdown (RopeBar)]] (for dip pattern)
- Push-ups [[EXL-83 Push-up (Standard & Variations)]]

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**
Research supports assisted training methods for developing strength in compound movements. Studies demonstrate that assisted exercises can effectively build strength patterns while allowing proper form practice at submaximal loads.

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**

**Programming Guidelines:**
- Begin with 60-80% assistance (counterweight = 60-80% of bodyweight)
- Aim for 3-4 sets of 6-12 repetitions
- Reduce assistance by 5-10 pounds weekly as strength improves
- Track assistance weight and reps to monitor progress

**Assessment Metrics [[O2. Measuring Fitness Progress]]:**
- Assistance weight used for specific reps/sets
- Reps performed at a given assistance level
- Progress towards less assistance or unassisted reps
- Qualitative assessment of form

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**
⚠️ **Over-relying on assistance:** Using too much help prevents strength development
⚠️ **Incomplete range of motion:** Not utilizing full movement despite machine assistance
⚠️ **Poor scapular control:** Allowing shoulders to elevate or protract excessively
⚠️ **Rushing the movement:** Not controlling both concentric and eccentric phases
⚠️ **Inconsistent foot/knee placement:** Unstable platform contact affects movement quality

### **EXL-65: Assisted Pull-up/Dip Machine**

**Conditions:** 
- Acute shoulder or elbow injuries
- Follow contraindications for unassisted pull-ups/dips

**Safety Considerations:**
- Ensure secure placement on assistance pad
- Select appropriate assistance level allowing good form through full ROM
- Control the movement; don't allow pad to push up too quickly
- Maintain stable core throughout movement
