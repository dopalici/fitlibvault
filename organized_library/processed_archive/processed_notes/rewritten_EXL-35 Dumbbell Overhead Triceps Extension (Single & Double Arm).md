### **EXL-35: Dumbbell Overhead Triceps Extension (Single & Double Arm)**
**ID:** EXL-35-DB-OHTRIEXT  
**Created:** 2025-04-29 (Updated: 2025-01-15)

## Quick Takeaway
An effective isolation exercise for building triceps strength and size, particularly targeting the long head through the overhead stretch position.

## Key Points
- Targets all three heads of the triceps, with special emphasis on the long head due to shoulder flexion
- Can be performed single or double arm, seated or standing for stability variations
- Focus on keeping upper arms vertical and controlling the weight through full range of motion
- Start with lighter weight to master form before progressing load
- Excellent for building triceps mass when combined with compound pressing movements

## Deep Dive

### Exercise Overview
The dumbbell overhead triceps extension is an isolation exercise targeting the triceps brachii, performed by extending the elbow(s) from a flexed position behind the head while holding a dumbbell overhead. The overhead position places the shoulder in flexion, which creates a significant stretch on the long head of the triceps - the only head that crosses both the shoulder and elbow joints.

**Difficulty Level:** Beginner to Intermediate

### Target Muscles and Biomechanics
**Primary Movers (Agonists):** Triceps Brachii (long head highly emphasized due to shoulder flexion position)
**Secondary Movers (Synergists):** Anconeus  
**Stabilizers:** Core musculature [[L4. Core Stability Training]], scapular stabilizers, rotator cuff

The overhead position is biomechanically significant because it places the long head of the triceps on stretch at the shoulder joint while allowing full range of motion at the elbow. This stretch-mediated hypertrophy stimulus may contribute to enhanced muscle growth (Baroni et al., 2017).

### Execution Technique

#### Setup (Double Arm)
- Sit or stand upright with core engaged [[A18. Proper Lifting Mechanics]]
- Hold one dumbbell vertically with both hands cupped under the top end (diamond grip)
- Raise dumbbell directly overhead with arms fully extended

#### Setup (Single Arm)  
- Sit or stand upright with core engaged
- Hold dumbbell in one hand, raise directly overhead with arm fully extended
- Optional: Use non-working hand to support elbow of working arm

#### Movement Execution
- Keep upper arm(s) vertical and close to head (avoid excessive elbow flare)
- Inhale and lower dumbbell behind head by bending only at the elbow(s)
- Lower as far as mobility allows while feeling stretch in triceps
- Exhale and extend elbow(s) fully, pressing dumbbell back to starting position
- Focus on triceps contraction while keeping upper arm(s) stable throughout

#### Breathing Pattern
- Inhale during lowering (eccentric phase)
- Exhale during extension (concentric phase)

## Research Evidence
Baroni et al. (2017). Muscle architecture adaptations to knee extensor eccentric training: Rectus femoris vs. vastus lateralis. Muscle & Nerve, 56(5), 956-963. DOI: 10.1002/mus.25592

Research on stretch-mediated hypertrophy supports the effectiveness of exercises that place muscles in lengthened positions, such as the overhead triceps extension. The overhead position maximizes the stretch on the long head of the triceps, potentially enhancing the hypertrophic stimulus.

## Practical Applications

### Programming Recommendations
- **Sets/Reps:** 3-4 sets of 8-15 reps for hypertrophy [[B3. Hypertrophy Training Principles]]
- **Frequency:** 2-3 times per week as part of arm or push training [[B5. Training Volume and Frequency]]
- **Load Selection:** Start with 60-70% of what you can handle for other triceps exercises

### Progression Strategies
- Increase load (dumbbell weight) following progressive overload principles [[B1. Progressive Overload]]
- Increase reps/sets for volume progression [[B5. Training Volume and Frequency]]
- Tempo variations: pause at stretch position, slow 3-4 second eccentrics [[B20. Eccentric Training]]
- Progress to barbell overhead triceps extension [[EXL-13 Barbell Skullcrusher (Lying Triceps Extension)]]
- Advance to cable overhead triceps extension for constant tension [[EXL-68 Cable Overhead Triceps Extension (RopeBar)]]

### Regression Options
- Decrease load
- Switch to triceps pushdowns [[EXL-67 Cable Triceps Pushdown (RopeBar)]]
- Use triceps kickbacks [[EXL-34 Dumbbell Triceps Kickback]]
- Resistance band overhead triceps extension [[B11. Resistance Bands]]
- Perform seated with back support for enhanced stability

## Common Mistakes
⚠️ **Excessive elbow flare** - Keep elbows close to head to maintain triceps emphasis and protect shoulders
⚠️ **Moving upper arms** - Using shoulders instead of isolating elbow extension reduces triceps activation
⚠️ **Incomplete range of motion** - Not achieving full stretch at bottom or complete extension at top
⚠️ **Lower back arching** - Especially when standing; engage core to maintain neutral spine
⚠️ **Too heavy too soon** - Control and form are more important than load for this isolation exercise

## Practical Applications
### Training Integration
- Perform after compound pressing movements when triceps are pre-fatigued
- Excellent as a finishing exercise for arm specialization programs
- Can be superset with biceps exercises for time-efficient arm training
- Use as part of pre-exhaustion protocol before compound pressing movements

### Equipment Considerations
Use quality dumbbells with secure collars [[B8. Free Weights (Barbells, Dumbbells)]]. Seated version provides more stability for beginners or when using heavier loads.

## Contraindications & Safety
### Conditions Requiring Caution
- Acute elbow or shoulder injuries, particularly impingement syndromes [[IX-6. Exercise Contraindications]]
- Limited shoulder flexion mobility - assess and improve flexibility first [[L2. Mobility and Flexibility Development]]
- History of elbow hyperextension injuries

### Safety Guidelines  
- Control weight carefully, especially when lowering behind head
- Keep elbows from flaring excessively to protect shoulder joint
- Use appropriate weight that allows strict form maintenance
- Consider seated version for enhanced stability and safety
