### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
A beginner-friendly machine exercise that isolates the triceps through either overhead extension or pushdown motions, providing stable resistance for building arm strength and size.

### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
- Choose between overhead (emphasizes long head) or pushdown variations based on goals
- Keep upper arms completely stationary - only elbows should move
- Start with 8-12 reps at moderate weight, focusing on full range of motion
- Excellent for beginners due to machine stability and safety
- Progress by adding weight, reps, or advancing to free weight triceps exercises

### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
This isolation exercise performed on [[B10. Machine Training]] equipment specifically targets the triceps brachii through single-joint elbow extension. The triceps brachii consists of three heads (long, lateral, and medial), with overhead variations providing greater stretch and activation of the long head due to its origin at the scapula (Maeo et al., 2023).

### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
**Pushdown Type:** Mimics cable pushdown motion with arms at sides, emphasizing the lateral and medial heads while maintaining shoulder stability.

**Overhead Type:** Replicates overhead extension patterns, providing greater stretch on the long head and requiring enhanced shoulder mobility [[L2. Mobility and Flexibility Development]].

### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
The machine's fixed path of motion eliminates the need for stabilization compared to free weights, allowing for focused triceps activation. Research indicates that isolation exercises like machine triceps extensions can produce similar hypertrophy responses to compound movements when volume is equated (Gentil et al., 2013).

- **Beginners:** 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps, 2-3x per week
- **Intermediate:** 3-4 sets of 10-15 reps with tempo variations
- **Load Selection:** Start with weight allowing 2-3 reps in reserve

### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
Following [[B1. Progressive Overload]] principles:
1. Increase load gradually (2.5-5 lbs per week)
2. Add reps within target range [[B5. Training Volume and Frequency]]
3. Implement tempo variations (2-second pause at contraction)
4. Progress to unilateral variations if available
5. Advance to free weights: [[EXL-35 Dumbbell Overhead Triceps Extension (Single & Double Arm)]], [[EXL-13 Barbell Skullcrusher (Lying Triceps Extension)]], or [[EXL-67 Cable Triceps Pushdown (RopeBar)]]

### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
⚠️ **Moving upper arms during execution** - destroys isolation and reduces triceps activation
⚠️ **Using momentum or bouncing** - reduces muscle tension and increases injury risk
⚠️ **Incomplete range of motion** - limits strength gains and muscle development
⚠️ **Excessive elbow flaring** on pushdown variations - can stress elbow joint
⚠️ **Shoulder shrugging** - compensates for inadequate triceps strength

### **EXL-59: Triceps Extension (Machine - Overhead or Pushdown type)**
