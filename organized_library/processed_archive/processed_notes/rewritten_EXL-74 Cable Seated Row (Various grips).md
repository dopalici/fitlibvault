### **EXL-74: Cable Seated Row (Various grips)**
A versatile beginner-friendly back exercise using cables that allows grip variations to target different areas of the back while providing constant tension throughout the movement.

### **EXL-74: Cable Seated Row (Various grips)**
- Excellent beginner exercise for building back strength and muscle with constant tension
- Different grips target different areas: close grip for lats/mid-back thickness, wide grip for upper back/rear delts
- Focus on pulling with your back muscles (scapular retraction first) rather than just your arms
- Keep torso upright and avoid using momentum - control the weight throughout the full range
- Progress by increasing weight, reps, or advancing to free-weight rowing variations

### **EXL-74: Cable Seated Row (Various grips)**
**Close Neutral Grip (V-bar):**
- Emphasizes latissimus dorsi and mid-back thickness
- Allows for greater range of motion
- Targets more vertical pulling pattern

**Wide Pronated Grip (Lat bar):**
- Emphasizes upper back, rear deltoids, and trapezius
- More horizontal pulling emphasis
- Better for developing back width

**Single D-Handles (Neutral/Rotating):**
- Allows greatest range of motion
- Enables unilateral focus to address imbalances
- Most natural grip position

Research on cable exercises demonstrates consistent muscle activation patterns compared to free weights, with the advantage of constant tension throughout the range of motion. 4. Sit upright with neutral spine, chest up

### **EXL-74: Cable Seated Row (Various grips)**
Following proper lifting mechanics [[A18. Proper Lifting Mechanics]]:

**Concentric Phase (Pull):**
- Initiate movement by retracting scapulae (pull shoulder blades together)
- Exhale while pulling handle toward torso
- Target pull location: lower abdomen (close grip) or mid/upper abdomen (wide grip)
- Keep torso relatively upright with minimal backward lean
- Drive elbows back behind torso
- Squeeze back muscles at peak contraction

**Eccentric Phase (Return):**
- Inhale while slowly controlling weight back to start position
- Allow scapulae to protract (stretch) at bottom
- Maintain tension throughout the negative portion

### **EXL-74: Cable Seated Row (Various grips)**
Implementing progressive overload [[B1. Progressive Overload]] and appropriate training volume [[B5. Training Volume and Frequency]]:

**Load Progression:**
- Increase weight stack resistance
- Add additional sets or repetitions

**Technical Progression:**
- Tempo variations (2-second pause at peak contraction)
- Slow eccentric emphasis [[B20. Eccentric Training]] (3-4 second negatives)
- Progress to unsupported free-weight rows: [[EXL-06 Barbell Bent-Over Row (Pronated Grip)]] or [[EXL-27 Dumbbell Row (Single Arm)]]

**Regression Options:**
- Decrease load/resistance
- Use chest-supported machine variations [[EXL-57 Seated Row (Machine - Close GripNeutral)]] or [[EXL-58 Chest Supported Row (Machine)]]
- Resistance band rows [[B11. Resistance Bands]] for minimal resistance

### **EXL-74: Cable Seated Row (Various grips)**
⚠️ **Excessive torso swing or momentum** - Keep torso relatively stable and pull with back muscles, not body momentum

⚠️ **Rounding the lower back** - Maintain neutral spine throughout the movement to prevent injury

⚠️ **Shoulder shrugging** - Focus on scapular retraction rather than elevating shoulders toward ears

⚠️ **Arm-dominant pulling** - Initiate movement with scapular retraction before arm flexion

⚠️ **Incomplete range of motion** - Ensure full scapular protraction at start and full retraction at finish

⚠️ **Pulling too high** - Target the appropriate body region based on grip selection and training goals
