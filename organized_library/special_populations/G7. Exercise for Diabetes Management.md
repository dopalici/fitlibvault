### Exercise for Diabetes Management
**ID:** G7 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Exercise for diabetes management encompasses evidence-based physical activity interventions that improve glycemic control, insulin sensitivity, and cardiovascular health in individuals with Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes. This approach integrates exercise prescription with blood glucose monitoring, medication timing, and nutritional considerations for optimal metabolic outcomes.
