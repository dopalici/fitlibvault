### Cardiovascular Disease Exercise Prescription
**ID:** G8 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Cardiovascular disease exercise prescription involves designing safe and effective exercise programs for individuals with heart conditions, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, and post-cardiac event recovery. This specialized approach emphasizes gradual progression, continuous monitoring, and multidisciplinary collaboration to improve cardiovascular health while minimizing risks.

- **Risk Stratification and Clearance:** Comprehensive assessment protocols to determine exercise safety and appropriate supervision levels, connecting to [[../health_conditions/IX-6. Exercise Contraindications]] and [[../key_overviews/Q4. Interpreting Biometric & Progress Data]]
- **Cardiac Rehabilitation Protocols:** Structured exercise progressions for post-event recovery and long-term cardiovascular health maintenance, linking to [[../foundation/A39. Cardiovascular Adaptations to Training]] and [[../foundation/A21. Principle of Progression]]
- **Heart Rate and Intensity Monitoring:** Precise exercise intensity prescription using heart rate zones, RPE, and other cardiovascular markers, relating to [[../cardiovascular_training/E13. Heart Rate Zone Training]] and [[../foundation/A8. Cardiovascular Physiology Fundamentals]]
- **Aerobic Base Development:** Emphasis on low-to-moderate intensity cardiovascular training for improved cardiac function, connecting to [[../cardiovascular_training/E2. Steady-State Cardio (LISS - Low-Intensity Steady State)]] and [[../cardiovascular_training/E11. VO2 Max Training]]
- **Resistance Training Considerations:** Safe integration of strength training with modified protocols for cardiac populations, linking to [[../resistance_training/B4. Strength Training Principles]] and [[../foundation/A19. Principle of Overload]]
