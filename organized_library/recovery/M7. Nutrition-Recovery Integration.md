### M7. Nutrition-Recovery Integration

Nutrition-recovery integration involves the strategic coordination of nutrient timing, composition, and delivery methods to optimize the body's repair and adaptation processes following exercise stress. This approach goes beyond basic post-workout nutrition to encompass comprehensive recovery-focused dietary strategies that support cellular repair, glycogen replenishment, and hormonal optimization.

**Core Components:**
- **Post-Exercise Nutrient Timing:** Strategic protein and carbohydrate delivery within optimal time windows to maximize muscle protein synthesis and glycogen resynthesis ([[../nutrition_diet/C3. Nutrient Timing]], [[../nutrition_diet/C29. Nutrition for Recovery]])
- **Recovery-Specific Macronutrient Strategies:** Tailored protein, carbohydrate, and fat ratios designed to support different phases of the recovery process ([[../nutrition_diet/C2. Macronutrient Distribution]], [[../resistance_training/B2. Protein Requirements for Muscle Growth]])
- **Anti-Inflammatory Nutrition Protocols:** Targeted use of foods and nutrients that modulate inflammation and oxidative stress during recovery ([[../nutrition_diet/C33. Anti-Inflammatory FoodsDiets]], [[../nutrition_diet/C34. Antioxidants]])
- **Hydration and Electrolyte Optimization:** Advanced fluid replacement strategies that account for sweat composition, environmental factors, and individual needs ([[../nutrition_diet/C4. Hydration]], [[../nutrition_diet/C5. Electrolytes]])
- **Recovery Supplementation Protocols:** Evidence-based use of specific supplements to enhance recovery processes and reduce time between quality training sessions ([[../nutrition_diet/C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]], [[../nutrition_diet/C24. Ergogenic Aids]])
