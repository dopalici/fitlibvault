### M9. Recovery Periodization

Recovery periodization involves the systematic planning and manipulation of recovery strategies throughout different phases of training to optimize adaptation, prevent overreaching, and peak performance at target times. This approach treats recovery as a trainable component that requires structured progression and strategic variation aligned with training demands and competition schedules.

**Core Components:**
- **Periodized Recovery Volume:** Systematic variation of recovery intervention frequency and intensity based on training phase and accumulated fatigue ([[../foundation/A25. Principle of Periodization]], [[M2. Overtraining and Functional Overreaching]])
- **Phase-Specific Recovery Strategies:** Tailored recovery protocols that match the physiological demands of different training blocks and competition periods ([[../foundation/A47. Training Periodization and Planning]], [[../elite_performance/K2. Advanced Periodization Strategies]])
- **Recovery Load Management:** Integration of recovery metrics with training load monitoring to guide adaptive recovery prescription ([[../foundation/A48. Training Load Monitoring]], [[../foundation/A53. Training Load Management]])
- **Tapering and Peak Recovery:** Specialized recovery protocols designed to optimize performance for competition through strategic fatigue dissipation ([[../foundation/A25. Principle of Periodization]], [[../elite_performance/K4. Advanced Periodization Models Explored]])
- **Long-Term Recovery Planning:** Multi-year recovery development strategies that account for training age, life stressors, and career periodization ([[../foundation/A23. Principle of Individuality]], [[../long_term_development/N2. Training for Longevity (Healthspan & Lifespan)]])
