# B57. Westside Conjugate Analysis

**ID:** B57-WESTSIDE-CONJUGATE-METHODS
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

Westside Conjugate training represents a comprehensive system that simultaneously develops multiple strength qualities through varied methods and exercises within the same training week. Developed by Louie Simmons, this approach utilizes max effort, dynamic effort, and repetition methods to address all aspects of strength development while preventing accommodation and maintaining year-round progress.

## Core Components

- **Conjugate Periodization:** Simultaneous training of multiple strength qualities (maximal, speed, and repetition strength)
- **Max Effort Method:** Weekly maximum singles in varied exercises to develop absolute strength
- **Dynamic Effort Method:** Speed work with submaximal loads to develop rate of force development and power
- **Repetition Method:** High-volume accessory work to address weaknesses and build muscle mass
- **Accommodation Resistance:** Use of bands and chains to alter strength curves and enhance power development
