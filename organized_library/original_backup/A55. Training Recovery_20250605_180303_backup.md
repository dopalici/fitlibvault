# A55. Microbiome-Exercise Interactions

**ID:** A55-MICROBIOME-EXERCISE-INTERACTIONS
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Microbiome-exercise interactions involve understanding and optimizing the bidirectional relationship between physical activity and gut microbiota, including assessment of microbial diversity, metabolic function, and performance optimization.

## Key Points

- Microbial diversity improves by 20-30% with targeted training
- Metabolic function increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Performance optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Recovery outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Immune function increases by 25-35% with appropriate management
