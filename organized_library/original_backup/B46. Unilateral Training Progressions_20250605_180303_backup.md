### B46. Unilateral Training Progressions
**ID:** B46-UNILATERAL-TRAINING-PROGRESSIONS
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Unilateral training progressions involve systematically developing single-limb strength, stability, and coordination through progressive exercise modifications. This approach addresses muscular imbalances, enhances functional movement patterns, and develops the unilateral strength and stability demands common in sports and daily activities.

**Core Components:**
- **Bilateral to Unilateral Progression:** Systematically transitioning from two-limb to single-limb exercises to develop independent limb strength and coordination
- **Stability Progression:** Advancing from stable bilateral support to unstable unilateral positions to challenge proprioception and stabilizing muscles
- **Load Progression:** Gradually increasing resistance in unilateral movements while maintaining proper form and bilateral strength balance
- **Contralateral Training Effects:** Utilizing cross-education phenomena where training one limb can provide modest strength benefits to the untrained contralateral limb
- **Asymmetry Assessment and Correction:** Identifying and addressing strength, mobility, or coordination imbalances between limbs through targeted unilateral training
