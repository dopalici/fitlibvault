# A40. Biomechanics of Deceleration

**ID:** A40-BIOMECHANICS-DECELERATION
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Biomechanics of deceleration involves understanding the mechanical principles and neuromuscular control required for safe and efficient movement deceleration, including force absorption, joint stability, and injury prevention.

## Key Points

- Deceleration mechanics improve by 20-30% with targeted training
- Force absorption increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Joint stability enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Performance outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
