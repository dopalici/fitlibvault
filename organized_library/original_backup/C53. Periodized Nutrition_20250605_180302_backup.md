### C53. Periodized Nutrition

**ID:** C53-PERIODIZED-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Periodized nutrition involves systematic manipulation of dietary variables in alignment with training phases to optimize performance, adaptation, and recovery throughout annual training cycles. This strategic approach coordinates macronutrient distribution, caloric intake, and supplement timing with specific training demands and competitive goals to maximize physiological adaptations and peak performance outcomes.

**Core Components:**

- **Training Phase Alignment:** Synchronizing nutritional strategies with distinct training phases including base building, intensity work, competition preparation, and recovery periods ([[A25. Principle of Periodization]], [[K2. Advanced Periodization Strategies]])
- **Macronutrient Periodization:** Systematically adjusting carbohydrate, protein, and fat intake based on training volume, intensity, and specific adaptation goals ([[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]], [[C17. Carb Cycling]])
- **Caloric Manipulation:** Strategic manipulation of energy intake to support training demands, body composition goals, and metabolic adaptations across different training phases ([[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]], [[C8. Metabolic Adaptation (Adaptive Thermogenesis)]])
- **Supplement Periodization:** Timing and cycling of supplements to align with specific training adaptations and avoid tolerance development ([[C20. Supplements (Overview & Framework)]], [[C24. Ergogenic Aids]])
- **Recovery Integration:** Coordinating nutrition protocols with planned recovery phases to maximize adaptation and prevent overtraining syndrome ([[M2. Overtraining and Functional Overreaching]], [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]])
