### Clinical Nutrition for Athletes
**ID:** C97 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Clinical nutrition for athletes involves the specialized application of nutritional therapy to address medical conditions, optimize recovery from injuries, and support athletes with chronic health conditions while maintaining peak performance. This approach integrates medical nutrition therapy principles with sports nutrition science to provide comprehensive care for athletes facing health challenges.

**Core Components:**
- **Medical Nutrition Therapy:** Implementing evidence-based nutritional interventions for athletes with diabetes, cardiovascular disease, gastrointestinal disorders, and other chronic conditions [[G3. Training with Health Limitations & Chronic Conditions]]
- **Injury Recovery Nutrition:** Designing specific nutritional protocols to accelerate healing, reduce inflammation, and support tissue repair following musculoskeletal injuries [[IX-3. Basic Injury Management (Acute Phase)]]
- **Disordered Eating Management:** Providing clinical nutrition support for athletes with eating disorders while maintaining competitive performance and health [[I3. Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S)]]
- **Supplement Interactions:** Managing potential interactions between therapeutic medications and sports supplements to ensure safety and efficacy [[C23. Supplement Safety, Regulation, and Quality Control]]
- **Laboratory Monitoring:** Interpreting clinical laboratory values, nutritional biomarkers, and health indicators to guide therapeutic nutrition interventions [[Q4. Interpreting Biometric & Progress Data]]
