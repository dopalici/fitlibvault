### B28. Conjugate Method
**ID:** B28-CONJUGATE-METHOD
**Created:** 2025-06-05

The Conjugate Method is a training system that simultaneously develops multiple strength qualities through the concurrent use of different training methods within the same microcycle. This approach was popularized by Westside Barbell for powerlifting performance.

**Key Components:**
- **Max Effort Method:** Working up to daily maximum loads (90-100% 1RM)
- **Dynamic Effort Method:** Speed work with submaximal loads (50-60% + bands/chains)
- **Repetition Method:** High-volume accessory work to muscular failure
- **Concurrent Development:** Simultaneous training of maximal strength and speed-strength
- **Exercise Rotation:** Frequent variation of main movements to prevent accommodation

**Training Applications:**
- Elite powerlifting performance enhancement
- Strength-power sport preparation
- Breaking through strength plateaus via varied stimuli
- Advanced training for experienced lifters
- Sports requiring maximal strength and explosive power
