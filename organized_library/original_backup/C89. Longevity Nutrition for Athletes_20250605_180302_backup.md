### Longevity Nutrition for Athletes
**ID:** C89 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Longevity nutrition for athletes focuses on dietary strategies that support both immediate performance goals and long-term health outcomes, emphasizing cellular health, cognitive function preservation, and disease prevention while maintaining competitive capabilities. This approach balances performance optimization with strategies that promote healthspan and lifespan extension.

**Core Components:**
- **Cellular Health Optimization:** Implementing [[C34. Antioxidants]] and anti-aging compounds through [[C30. Food Quality & Health (Whole vs Processed)]] to combat cellular damage and support mitochondrial function
- **Cognitive Function Preservation:** Utilizing [[C61. Cognitive Nutrition for Athletes]] strategies that support brain health and maintain decision-making capabilities throughout an athletic career
- **Metabolic Health Maintenance:** Applying [[C62. Metabolic Flexibility Training]] and [[C8. Metabolic Adaptation (Adaptive Thermogenesis)]] principles to preserve insulin sensitivity and metabolic function
- **Inflammatory Management:** Long-term implementation of [[C33. Anti-Inflammatory FoodsDiets]] and [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]] optimization to reduce chronic inflammation
- **Age-Appropriate Adaptations:** Integrating [[C66. Masters Athlete Nutrition (45+ years)]] principles with [[N2. Training for Longevity (Healthspan & Lifespan)]] to adjust nutrition strategies across career phases
