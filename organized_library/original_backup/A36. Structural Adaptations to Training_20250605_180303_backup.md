# A36. Asymmetry Assessment and Correction

**ID:** A36-ASYMMETRY-ASSESSMENT-CORRECTION
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Asymmetry assessment and correction involves understanding and addressing differences in movement patterns, strength, and function between sides of the body, including systematic evaluation and targeted interventions to improve symmetry and performance.

## Key Points

- Asymmetry identification improves by 20-30% with targeted assessment
- Correction effectiveness increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Movement symmetry enhances by 25-35% with optimal interventions
- Performance outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic correction
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
