# A63. pH Regulation Mechanisms

**ID:** A63-PH-REGULATION-MECHANISMS
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

pH regulation mechanisms involve understanding and optimizing the body's acid-base balance during exercise, including assessment of buffering capacity, acid-base homeostasis, and performance optimization.

## Key Points

- Buffering capacity improves by 20-30% with targeted training
- Acid-base balance increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Performance optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Recovery outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Endurance capacity increases by 25-35% with appropriate management
