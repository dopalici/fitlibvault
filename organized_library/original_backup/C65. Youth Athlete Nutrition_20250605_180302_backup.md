### Youth Athlete Nutrition
**ID:** C65 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Youth athlete nutrition addresses the unique nutritional needs of developing athletes, balancing the demands of growth, development, and athletic performance. This specialized approach considers the physiological, psychological, and social factors that influence young athletes' nutritional needs while establishing healthy long-term eating patterns and performance habits.

**Core Components:**
- **Growth and Development Nutrition:** Meeting the increased energy and nutrient needs for normal growth while supporting athletic training demands, building on [[G2. Age-Specific Exercise Recommendations]] and [[A4. Micronutrients]]
- **Energy Availability for Young Athletes:** Ensuring adequate energy intake to support growth, training, and recovery while preventing relative energy deficiency, connected to [[I3. Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S)]] and [[A1. Energy Balance]]
- **Hydration and Thermoregulation Considerations:** Understanding the unique thermoregulatory challenges young athletes face and appropriate hydration strategies, linking to [[C4. Hydration]] and [[A27. Thermoregulation During Exercise]]
- **Supplement Safety and Appropriateness:** Evidence-based guidance on supplement use in youth athletes with emphasis on food-first approaches, incorporating [[C23. Supplement Safety, Regulation, and Quality Control]] and [[C30. Food Quality & Health (Whole vs Processed)]]
- **Behavioral and Educational Components:** Developing healthy eating behaviors and nutrition education appropriate for different developmental stages, connected to [[F4. Behavioral Psychology in Fitness]] and [[N3. Building Sustainable Fitness Habits]]
