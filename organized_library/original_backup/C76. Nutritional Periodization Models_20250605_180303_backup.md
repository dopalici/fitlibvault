### Nutritional Periodization Models
**ID:** C76 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Nutritional periodization involves systematically varying nutrient intake and timing to align with training phases and optimize performance adaptations. This strategic approach matches macronutrient distribution, caloric intake, and supplement protocols to specific training demands throughout annual, mesocycle, and microcycle phases.

**Core Components:**
- **Macrocycle Nutrition Planning:** Annual nutrition strategy aligned with [[A25. Principle of Periodization]] and [[K2. Advanced Periodization Strategies]] to support long-term performance goals
- **Training Phase-Specific Fueling:** Adjusting [[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]] and [[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]] based on training intensity, volume, and adaptation goals
- **Competition Preparation Protocols:** Strategic [[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]] and [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] modifications for peak performance windows
- **Recovery-Focused Nutrition Cycles:** Implementing [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]] strategies during deload phases and [[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]] periods
- **Body Composition Periodization:** Coordinated approach with [[H1. Body Composition Assessment]] to optimize [[C26. Nutrition for Fat Loss]] and [[C25. Nutrition for Muscle Gain (Hypertrophy)]] phases
