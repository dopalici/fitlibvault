### Disordered Eating Prevention
**ID:** C81 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Disordered eating prevention in athletes involves recognizing early warning signs, implementing protective nutritional practices, and fostering healthy relationships with food to prevent the development of eating disorders and relative energy deficiency in sport (RED-S). This approach emphasizes balanced nutrition education, psychological support, and sustainable dietary practices.

**Core Components:**
- **Early Warning Sign Recognition:** Identifying behaviors and attitudes that may lead to [[I3. Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S)]] and other eating-related performance complications
- **Balanced Nutrition Education:** Teaching sustainable approaches from [[C19. Intuitive Eating]] and [[D2. Diet Adherence and Behavior Change]] rather than restrictive dietary practices
- **Body Image and Performance Relationship:** Understanding connections between [[P3. Body Image and Fitness Culture]] and healthy performance nutrition without excessive focus on appearance
- **Psychological Support Integration:** Incorporating [[F4. Behavioral Psychology in Fitness]] principles and stress management techniques to address underlying psychological factors
- **Sustainable Energy Availability:** Ensuring adequate [[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]] and [[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]] to support training demands while maintaining metabolic health
