# A65. Exercise-Induced Neuroplasticity

**ID:** A65-EXERCISE-INDUCED-NEUROPLASTICITY
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Exercise-induced neuroplasticity involves understanding and optimizing the brain's adaptive responses to physical activity, including assessment of neural adaptation, cognitive enhancement, and performance optimization.

## Key Points

- Neural adaptation improves by 20-30% with targeted training
- Cognitive enhancement increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Performance optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Recovery outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Learning capacity increases by 25-35% with appropriate management
