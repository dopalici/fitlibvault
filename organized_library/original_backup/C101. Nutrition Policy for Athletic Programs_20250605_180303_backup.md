### Nutrition Policy for Athletic Programs
**ID:** C101 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Nutrition policy for athletic programs encompasses the development, implementation, and management of comprehensive nutrition guidelines and protocols within organized sports settings. Effective nutrition policies ensure consistent, evidence-based practices while addressing safety, performance optimization, and regulatory compliance across diverse athletic populations and competitive levels.

**Core Components:**
- **Policy Development Framework:** Creating comprehensive nutrition policies that address supplement use, meal planning, hydration protocols, and emergency nutrition management [[C23. Supplement Safety, Regulation, and Quality Control]]
- **Educational Standards:** Establishing mandatory nutrition education requirements for coaches, athletes, and support staff to ensure consistent knowledge application [[P4. Evidence-Based Coaching Methodologies]]
- **Risk Management Protocols:** Implementing systems to identify and manage nutrition-related risks including supplement contamination, food allergies, and disordered eating [[I3. Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S)]]
- **Performance Monitoring Systems:** Developing standardized protocols for tracking nutrition compliance, performance outcomes, and program effectiveness [[O2. Measuring Fitness Progress]]
- **Ethical Guidelines:** Establishing clear ethical standards for nutrition practices, including informed consent, privacy protection, and conflict of interest management [[P1. Ethical Training and Coaching]]
