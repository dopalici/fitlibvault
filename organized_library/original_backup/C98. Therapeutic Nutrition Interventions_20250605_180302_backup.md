### Therapeutic Nutrition Interventions
**ID:** C98 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Therapeutic nutrition interventions encompass targeted nutritional strategies designed to address specific physiological dysfunctions, optimize metabolic pathways, and support therapeutic outcomes in athletic populations. These interventions go beyond basic sports nutrition to include clinical-grade protocols for managing inflammation, metabolic dysfunction, and performance-limiting conditions.

**Core Components:**
- **Anti-Inflammatory Protocols:** Implementing omega-3 fatty acids, polyphenol-rich foods, and specialized anti-inflammatory diets to manage chronic inflammation and support recovery [[C33. Anti-Inflammatory Foods/Diets]]
- **Metabolic Restoration:** Using targeted nutritional interventions to restore metabolic flexibility, improve insulin sensitivity, and optimize energy metabolism [[C8. Metabolic Adaptation (Adaptive Thermogenesis)]]
- **Gut Health Optimization:** Employing prebiotics, probiotics, and therapeutic nutrition to restore intestinal barrier function and optimize nutrient absorption [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]]
- **Hormonal Modulation:** Utilizing specific nutrients and dietary patterns to support healthy hormone production and regulation in athletes [[I1. Training and Hormonal Responses]]
- **Oxidative Stress Management:** Implementing antioxidant-rich interventions and targeted supplementation to manage exercise-induced oxidative stress [[C34. Antioxidants]]
