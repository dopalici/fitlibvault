### Travel Nutrition for Athletes
**ID:** C70 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Athletic travel presents unique nutritional challenges including limited food access, time zone disruptions, and unfamiliar food environments. Proper planning and adaptation strategies are essential for maintaining performance standards and supporting recovery during competition travel.

**Core Components:**
- **Pre-Travel Preparation:** Strategic meal planning, portable nutrition supplies, and research of destination food options to maintain [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] and performance nutrition protocols
- **Hydration Management:** Addressing dehydration risks from air travel and climate changes while managing [[C4. Hydration]] needs across different environments and altitudes
- **Jet Lag Nutrition:** Using meal timing and specific nutrients to support circadian rhythm adjustment and maintain [[F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]] for optimal recovery
- **Food Safety Protocols:** Risk assessment and safe food handling practices to prevent gastrointestinal illness that could impact [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]] and performance
- **Competition Day Logistics:** Portable, familiar nutrition strategies that can be implemented regardless of location, maintaining consistency with [[C5. Pre, During, and Post-Exercise Nutrition]] protocols
