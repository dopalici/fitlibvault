### Budget-Conscious Sports Nutrition
**ID:** C71 **Created:** 2025-06-05

High-quality sports nutrition can be achieved on limited budgets through strategic food choices, bulk purchasing, and prioritizing nutrient-dense whole foods. Cost-effective approaches can still meet all performance nutrition requirements without compromising training adaptations or recovery.

**Core Components:**
- **Economical Protein Sources:** Utilizing affordable, complete proteins like eggs, legumes, and bulk chicken to meet [[B2. Protein Requirements for Muscle Growth]] without excessive cost
- **Bulk Carbohydrate Staples:** Strategic purchasing of rice, oats, and seasonal produce to support [[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]] fueling at minimal expense
- **Supplement Prioritization:** Focusing spending on evidence-based supplements from [[C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]] while avoiding expensive, unnecessary products
- **Meal Planning Efficiency:** Batch cooking and strategic meal preparation to reduce food waste and optimize [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] while minimizing costs
- **Whole Food Focus:** Emphasizing nutrient-dense, minimally processed foods that provide better value than expensive specialty sports products while supporting [[C30. Food Quality & Health (Whole vs Processed)]]
