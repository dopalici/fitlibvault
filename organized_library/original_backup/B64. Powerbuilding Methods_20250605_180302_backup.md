# B64. Powerbuilding Methods

**ID:** B64-POWERBUILDING-STRENGTH-HYPERTROPHY
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

Powerbuilding represents a hybrid training approach that combines powerlifting principles with bodybuilding methods to simultaneously develop maximum strength and muscle hypertrophy. This methodology integrates heavy compound movements for strength development with higher-volume accessory work for muscle growth, appealing to athletes seeking both strength and aesthetic improvements.

## Core Components

- **Dual Goal Integration:** Systematic combination of strength-focused and hypertrophy-focused training within the same program
- **Compound Movement Foundation:** Primary emphasis on squat, bench press, and deadlift for strength development
- **Hypertrophy Accessory Work:** Strategic inclusion of higher-volume isolation and assistance exercises for muscle growth
- **Periodization Balance:** Cycling between strength-emphasis and hypertrophy-emphasis phases
- **Progressive Overload Application:** Applying overload principles to both strength and hypertrophy components
