# A59. Myokine Signaling

**ID:** A59-MYOKINE-SIGNALING

## Quick Takeaway

Myokine signaling involves understanding and optimizing the communication between muscle tissue and other organs through exercise-induced cytokines, including assessment of inter-tissue communication, metabolic regulation, and performance optimization.
