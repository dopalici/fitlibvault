# A45. Biomechanics of Aging

**ID:** A45-BIOMECHANICS-AGING
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Biomechanics of aging involves understanding the changes in movement patterns, joint mechanics, and neuromuscular control that occur with advancing age, including assessment of functional capacity, movement efficiency, and injury prevention.

## Key Points

- Movement quality improves by 20-30% with targeted training
- Joint mechanics increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Performance optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Performance outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
