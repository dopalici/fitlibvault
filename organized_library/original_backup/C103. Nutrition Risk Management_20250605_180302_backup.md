### Nutrition Risk Management
**ID:** C103 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Nutrition risk management encompasses the systematic identification, assessment, and mitigation of potential risks associated with nutritional interventions in athletic populations. This comprehensive approach protects athlete health and performance while ensuring program compliance, reducing liability, and maintaining the integrity of nutrition support services.

**Core Components:**
- **Supplement Contamination Prevention:** Implementing rigorous screening protocols for supplement safety, third-party testing verification, and banned substance monitoring [[C23. Supplement Safety, Regulation, and Quality Control]]
- **Food Safety Protocols:** Establishing comprehensive food handling, storage, and preparation guidelines to prevent foodborne illness and contamination [[C4. Hydration]]
- **Allergy and Intolerance Management:** Developing systems to identify, document, and accommodate food allergies, intolerances, and dietary restrictions in team settings [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]]
- **Disordered Eating Prevention:** Creating early detection systems and intervention protocols for eating disorders and relative energy deficiency in sport [[I3. Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S)]]
- **Emergency Response Planning:** Establishing clear protocols for nutrition-related medical emergencies including severe allergic reactions and metabolic complications [[IX-6. Exercise Contraindications]]
