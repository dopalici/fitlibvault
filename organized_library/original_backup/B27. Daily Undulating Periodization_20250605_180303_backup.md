### B27. Daily Undulating Periodization
**ID:** B27-DAILY-UNDULATING-PERIODIZATION
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Daily Undulating Periodization (DUP) is a training system that varies intensity and volume on a daily or session-to-session basis rather than following traditional linear progression. This approach prevents adaptation plateaus through constant stimulus variation.

**Key Components:**
- **Daily Variation:** Frequent changes in intensity, volume, and exercise selection
- **Multiple Qualities:** Simultaneous development of strength, power, and hypertrophy
- **Undulating Pattern:** Non-linear progression with planned variation
- **Recovery Integration:** Built-in variation prevents overreaching
- **Flexible Programming:** Adaptable to individual responses and constraints

**Training Applications:**
- Advanced strength athletes seeking continued progress
- Sports requiring multiple physical qualities simultaneously
- Breaking through training plateaus with traditional periodization
- Time-efficient training for recreational athletes
- Concurrent training for strength and endurance athletes
