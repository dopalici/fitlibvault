# B55. Sheiko Programming

**ID:** B55-SHEIKO-VOLUME-PERIODIZATION
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

Sheiko programming represents a systematic approach to powerlifting training characterized by high volume, moderate intensities, and precise percentage-based loading schemes. Developed by Boris Sheiko, this methodology emphasizes volume accumulation through the 70-85% intensity range to build strength endurance and technical proficiency while strategically peaking for competition.

## Core Components

- **Percentage-Based Loading:** Strict adherence to predetermined percentage ranges with systematic progression schemes
- **High Volume Accumulation:** Extensive training volume in the 70-85% range to build strength base and work capacity
- **Competition Movement Priority:** Primary focus on squat, bench press, and deadlift with strategic accessory selection
- **Structured Periodization:** Clear phases of volume accumulation, intensity increases, and competition peaking
- **Technical Refinement:** Emphasis on movement quality and consistency through high-repetition practice
