# B54. Norwegian Method

**ID:** B54-NORWEGIAN-FREQUENCY-INTENSITY
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

The Norwegian Method combines high-frequency training with moderate to high intensities, emphasizing technical proficiency and consistent practice of competition movements. This approach balances the intensity demands of Bulgarian-style training with more sustainable volume and recovery protocols, making it accessible to a broader range of athletes while maintaining effectiveness.

## Core Components

- **Moderate-High Frequency:** Training primary movements 4-6 times per week with varied intensity zones
- **Technical Emphasis:** Prioritizing movement quality and technical consistency across all training sessions
- **Structured Intensity Distribution:** Systematic cycling between heavy, moderate, and technique-focused sessions
- **Competition Movement Focus:** Concentration on squat, bench press, and deadlift with minimal accessory work
- **Sustainable Recovery Protocols:** Built-in recovery mechanisms to prevent overreaching while maintaining high frequency
