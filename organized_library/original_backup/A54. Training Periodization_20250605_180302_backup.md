# A54. Neurotransmitter Optimization

**ID:** A54-NEUROTRANSMITTER-OPTIMIZATION
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Neurotransmitter optimization involves understanding and enhancing the balance of key neurotransmitters in response to exercise, including dopamine, serotonin, norepinephrine, and their impact on performance and recovery.

## Key Points

- Neurotransmitter balance improves by 20-30% with targeted training
- Cognitive function increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Performance optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Recovery outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Mental fatigue decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
