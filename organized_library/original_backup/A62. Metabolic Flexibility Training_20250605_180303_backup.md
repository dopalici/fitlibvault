# A62. Metabolic Flexibility Training

**ID:** A62-METABOLIC-FLEXIBILITY-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Metabolic flexibility training involves optimizing the body's ability to switch between different fuel sources efficiently, including assessment of substrate utilization, energy metabolism, and performance optimization.

## Key Points

- Substrate switching improves by 20-30% with targeted training
- Energy efficiency increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Performance optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Recovery outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Endurance capacity increases by 25-35% with appropriate management
