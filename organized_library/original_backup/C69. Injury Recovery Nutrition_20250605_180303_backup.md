### Injury Recovery Nutrition
**ID:** C69 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Nutrition plays a critical role in tissue repair, inflammation management, and recovery from athletic injuries. Strategic nutritional interventions can accelerate healing processes while maintaining muscle mass and supporting immune function during periods of reduced activity.

**Core Components:**
- **Enhanced Protein Requirements:** Increased protein intake (2.0-2.5g/kg) to support tissue repair and prevent muscle atrophy during immobilization, building on [[B2. Protein Requirements for Muscle Growth]] principles
- **Anti-Inflammatory Nutrition:** Strategic use of omega-3 fatty acids, polyphenols, and specific nutrients to modulate inflammation and support [[C33. Anti-Inflammatory FoodsDiets]] for optimal healing
- **Micronutrient Support:** Targeted supplementation of vitamin C, zinc, vitamin D, and other nutrients critical for collagen synthesis and [[A42. Connective Tissue Adaptations to Training]]
- **Energy Balance Management:** Adjusting caloric intake to prevent excessive weight gain while ensuring adequate energy for healing processes and maintaining [[A1. Energy Balance]]
- **Hydration and Circulation:** Optimizing fluid intake to support nutrient delivery and waste removal from injured tissues, connecting to [[C4. Hydration]] principles
