# B56. 5/3/1 Methodology

**ID:** B56-531-PROGRESSION-SYSTEM
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

The 5/3/1 methodology is a systematic strength training program that emphasizes slow, steady progression through predetermined percentage-based cycles. Created by Jim Wendler, this approach prioritizes long-term strength development through conservative loading, built-in progression mechanisms, and extensive accessory work to address weaknesses and build muscle mass.

## Core Components

- **Wave Periodization:** Four-week cycles with systematic intensity progression (65%, 75%, 85% weeks plus deload)
- **Conservative Progression:** Slow, sustainable increases based on training maximums rather than true maximums
- **Accessory Integration:** Structured supplemental and assistance work to address weaknesses and build muscle
- **Autoregulation Elements:** AMRAP (As Many Reps As Possible) sets to gauge readiness and drive adaptation
- **Long-Term Focus:** Emphasis on consistent progress over multiple training cycles rather than short-term gains
