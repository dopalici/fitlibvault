### B31. Overreaching Protocols
**ID:** B31-OVERREACHING-PROTOCOLS
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Overreaching protocols involve planned periods of intensified training that temporarily exceed an athlete's recovery capacity, followed by strategic recovery phases. This approach aims to achieve supercompensation and performance breakthroughs through controlled stress application.

**Key Components:**
- **Functional Overreaching:** Planned short-term performance decrement with rapid recovery
- **Intensification Phase:** Increased training volume, intensity, or frequency beyond normal capacity
- **Recovery Monitoring:** Careful tracking of physiological and performance markers
- **Taper Strategy:** Systematic reduction in training load to realize performance gains
- **Individual Tolerance:** Customization based on athlete's recovery capacity and training history

**Training Applications:**
- Pre-competition peaking protocols for elite athletes
- Breaking through performance plateaus via strategic overload
- Periodization strategy for strength and power sports
- Research tool for understanding adaptation mechanisms
- Advanced training for highly conditioned athletes
