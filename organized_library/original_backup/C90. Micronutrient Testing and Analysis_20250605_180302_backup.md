### Micronutrient Testing and Analysis
**ID:** C90 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Micronutrient testing and analysis involves comprehensive assessment of vitamin, mineral, and trace element status through laboratory testing, functional assessments, and symptom evaluation to identify deficiencies, imbalances, or suboptimal levels that may impact athletic performance. This data-driven approach enables precise nutritional interventions tailored to individual biochemical needs.

**Core Components:**
- **Comprehensive Laboratory Assessment:** Utilizing blood, urine, and tissue testing to evaluate [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]] status including vitamins, minerals, and functional markers
- **Functional Testing Integration:** Incorporating metabolic assessments that reveal how micronutrients impact [[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]] and cellular function rather than just serum levels
- **Performance Correlation Analysis:** Connecting micronutrient status with [[O2. Measuring Fitness Progress]] and identifying specific deficiencies that limit performance outcomes
- **Individualized Supplementation Protocols:** Developing targeted [[C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]] strategies based on testing results and avoiding unnecessary supplementation
- **Monitoring and Reassessment:** Establishing systematic retesting schedules to track intervention effectiveness and adjust protocols using [[C54. Precision Nutrition Monitoring]] principles
