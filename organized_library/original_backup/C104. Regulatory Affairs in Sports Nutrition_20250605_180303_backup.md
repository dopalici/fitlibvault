### Regulatory Affairs in Sports Nutrition
**ID:** C104 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Regulatory affairs in sports nutrition involves navigating the complex landscape of legal requirements, anti-doping regulations, and industry standards that govern nutritional products and practices in competitive athletics. Understanding regulatory frameworks is essential for ensuring compliance, protecting athletes from banned substances, and maintaining the integrity of competitive sport.

**Core Components:**
- **Anti-Doping Compliance:** Navigating WADA regulations, prohibited substance lists, and therapeutic use exemptions to ensure supplement and nutrition protocol compliance [[C23. Supplement Safety, Regulation, and Quality Control]]
- **FDA and International Regulations:** Understanding dietary supplement regulations, health claims, and labeling requirements across different jurisdictions [[C31. Reading Nutrition Labels]]
- **Third-Party Certification:** Implementing NSF, Informed Sport, and other certification programs to verify supplement safety and purity [[C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]]
- **Documentation and Reporting:** Maintaining comprehensive records for regulatory audits, adverse event reporting, and compliance verification [[P1. Ethical Training and Coaching]]
- **Policy Updates and Communication:** Staying current with evolving regulations and effectively communicating changes to athletes, coaches, and support staff [[J3. Misinformation in Fitness]]
