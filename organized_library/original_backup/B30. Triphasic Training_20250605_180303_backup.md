### B30. Triphasic Training
**ID:** B30-TRIPHASIC-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Triphasic Training is a systematic approach that separately develops the three phases of movement: eccentric, isometric, and concentric contractions. This method emphasizes optimal development of each phase to maximize overall athletic performance and movement efficiency.

**Key Components:**
- **Eccentric Phase:** Focused development of lengthening contractions for force absorption
- **Isometric Phase:** Static strength development at specific joint angles
- **Concentric Phase:** Explosive strength development for force production
- **Sequential Development:** Progressive emphasis on each phase across training blocks
- **Movement Integration:** Application of enhanced phase-specific strength to sport movements

**Training Applications:**
- Athletic performance enhancement across multiple sports
- Injury prevention through improved eccentric strength
- Rehabilitation protocols for movement dysfunction
- Youth athlete development with movement quality focus
- Breaking through performance plateaus via phase-specific training
