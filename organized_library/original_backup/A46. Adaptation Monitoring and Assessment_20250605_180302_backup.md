# A46. Autonomic Nervous System Training

**ID:** A46-AUTONOMIC-NERVOUS-SYSTEM-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Autonomic nervous system training involves understanding and optimizing the balance between sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous system activity, including assessment of heart rate variability, stress response, and recovery optimization.

## Key Points

- HRV improves by 20-30% with targeted training
- Stress management increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Recovery optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Performance outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
