# B53. Bulgarian Method

**ID:** B53-BULGARIAN-DAILY-MAX-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

The Bulgarian Method is a high-frequency, high-intensity training system originally developed for Olympic weightlifting that emphasizes daily training to maximum or near-maximum loads. This approach challenges conventional periodization by maintaining consistently high intensities while relying on frequent exposure to heavy loads to drive neural adaptations and skill refinement.

## Core Components

- **Daily Maximum Training:** Working up to daily maximums (90-105% of current max) across primary movement patterns
- **High Frequency Exposure:** Training the same movements multiple times per week to enhance motor learning and neural efficiency
- **Autoregulation Principles:** Daily load selection based on readiness, fatigue levels, and performance capacity
- **Minimal Accessory Work:** Focus on competition movements with limited supplemental exercises
- **Recovery Through Movement:** Using lighter training sessions and movement quality as active recovery methods
