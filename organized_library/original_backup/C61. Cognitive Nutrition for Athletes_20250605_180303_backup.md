### Cognitive Nutrition for Athletes
**ID:** C61 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Cognitive nutrition for athletes focuses on optimizing brain function, decision-making, and mental performance through targeted nutritional strategies. This approach recognizes that athletic performance depends not only on physical capabilities but also on cognitive functions such as reaction time, focus, decision-making speed, and mental resilience under pressure.

**Core Components:**
- **Neurotransmitter Support:** Nutritional strategies to optimize dopamine, serotonin, and acetylcholine production for enhanced focus and mood, building on [[A4. Micronutrients]] and [[F4. Behavioral Psychology in Fitness]]
- **Brain Energy Metabolism:** Understanding how glucose availability and ketone utilization affect cognitive performance during exercise, connected to [[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]] and [[C13. Ketogenic Diet]]
- **Stress Response Modulation:** Nutritional interventions to manage cortisol and other stress hormones that affect cognitive function, linking to [[I1. Training and Hormonal Responses]] and [[M3. Stress Management and Performance]]
- **Sleep-Cognition Interactions:** How nutrition affects sleep quality and subsequent cognitive performance, incorporating [[F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]] and [[C62. Sleep-Nutrition Interactions]]
- **Sport-Specific Cognitive Demands:** Tailoring nutrition strategies to meet the specific cognitive demands of different sports and positions, connected to [[A20. Principle of Specificity (SAID)]] and [[K3. Psychological Aspects of Elite Performance]]
