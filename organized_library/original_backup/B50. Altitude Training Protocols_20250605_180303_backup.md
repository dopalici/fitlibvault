### B50. Altitude Training Protocols
**ID:** B50-ALTITUDE-TRAINING-PROTOCOLS
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Altitude training protocols involve systematic exposure to reduced atmospheric pressure and oxygen availability to stimulate physiological adaptations that enhance endurance performance. These protocols utilize various altitude exposure strategies including live high-train high, live high-train low, and intermittent hypoxic training to optimize adaptation while managing the challenges of reduced training capacity at altitude.

**Core Components:**
- **Live High-Train High (LHTH):** Extended residence and training at moderate to high altitude (1500-3000m) to maximize erythropoietic and cardiovascular adaptations
- **Live High-Train Low (LHTL):** Residing at altitude for adaptation benefits while training at sea level to maintain high-intensity exercise capacity
- **Intermittent Hypoxic Exposure (IHE):** Short-duration altitude exposure sessions combined with sea-level training to trigger adaptations without compromising training quality
- **Acclimatization Protocols:** Systematic altitude exposure progression to minimize altitude sickness and optimize physiological adaptation timeline
- **Performance Monitoring:** Tracking oxygen saturation, heart rate, training capacity, and performance markers to optimize altitude exposure and prevent negative adaptations
