# B70. Ballistic Training Systems

**ID:** B70-BALLISTIC-EXPLOSIVE-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

Ballistic training systems focus on explosive movements where the resistance is accelerated throughout the entire range of motion, often resulting in the implement or body becoming airborne. These training methods maximize power development by eliminating deceleration phases common in traditional resistance training, enabling athletes to express force at high velocities.

## Core Components

- **Maximal Acceleration Training:** Movements that maintain acceleration throughout the full range of motion
- **Velocity-Specific Development:** Training at high velocities to enhance power at specific movement speeds
- **Implement Projection:** Exercises involving throwing, jumping, or releasing implements for maximum acceleration
- **Deceleration Elimination:** Removing the need to slow down implements at end range of motion
- **Sport-Specific Power Transfer:** Ballistic movements that closely match competitive movement patterns
