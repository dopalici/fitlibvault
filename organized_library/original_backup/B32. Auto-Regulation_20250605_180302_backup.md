### B32. Auto-Regulation
**ID:** B32-AUTO-REGULATION
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Auto-regulation is a training methodology that adjusts training variables in real-time based on an athlete's current physiological and psychological readiness to train. This approach uses subjective and objective feedback to modify intensity, volume, or exercise selection on a session-by-session basis, optimizing training stimulus while minimizing overreaching.

**Core Components:**
- **RPE-Based Programming:** Using Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE) or Repetitions in Reserve (RIR) to adjust training loads based on daily readiness and performance capacity
- **Readiness Assessment:** Implementing pre-training assessments (HRV, subjective wellness, performance tests) to guide training modifications
- **Flexible Load Adjustment:** Modifying planned training loads up or down based on real-time performance indicators and recovery status
- **Volume Titration:** Adjusting training volume (sets, reps) based on fatigue accumulation and performance quality within the session
- **Exercise Substitution:** Replacing planned exercises with alternatives based on pain, mobility restrictions, or equipment availability
