### Rest-Pause Training Protocols
**ID:** B74 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Rest-pause training involves brief intra-set rest periods to extend training volume beyond normal failure points, maximizing muscle fiber recruitment and metabolic stress. This high-intensity technique utilizes the rapid restoration of phosphocreatine stores to enable additional repetitions within a single extended set.

**Core Components:**
- **Phosphocreatine Recovery Timing:** Optimal rest intervals for energy system restoration using [[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]] and [[A7. Muscle Physiology Fundamentals]]
- **Failure Point Management:** Strategic approaches to reaching and extending muscular failure incorporating [[B3. Hypertrophy Training Principles]] and [[A19. Principle of Overload]]
- **Volume Accumulation Strategies:** Methods for maximizing training stimulus within extended sets using [[B5. Training Volume and Frequency]] and [[A36. Structural Adaptations to Training]]
- **Exercise Selection Guidelines:** Choosing appropriate movements for rest-pause application based on [[B4. Strength Training Principles]] and [[B8. Free Weights (Barbells, Dumbbells)]]
- **Recovery Management:** Balancing intensification with adequate restoration using [[B6. Rest and Recovery for Optimal Adaptation (Resistance Training Focus)]] and [[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]]
