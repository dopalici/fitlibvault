# A49. Circadian Rhythm Optimization

**ID:** A49-CIRCADIAN-RHYTHM-OPTIMIZATION
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Circadian rhythm optimization involves understanding and enhancing the body's natural 24-hour biological cycles, including sleep-wake patterns, hormone regulation, and performance timing for optimal adaptation and recovery.

## Key Points

- Sleep quality improves by 20-30% with proper timing
- Hormone regulation increases by 15-25% with optimal scheduling
- Performance timing enhances by 25-35% with circadian alignment
- Recovery outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
