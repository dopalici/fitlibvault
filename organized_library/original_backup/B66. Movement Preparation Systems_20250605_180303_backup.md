# B66. Movement Preparation Systems

**ID:** B66-MOVEMENT-PREP-WARMUP-SYSTEMS
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

Movement preparation systems encompass comprehensive warm-up protocols designed to optimize neuromuscular function, movement quality, and injury prevention before training or competition. These systematic approaches progress from general activation through movement-specific preparation to performance readiness, ensuring optimal physiological and neurological preparation for demanding activities.

## Core Components

- **Progressive Activation:** Systematic progression from general movement to specific movement preparation
- **Neuromuscular Preparation:** Activation of key stabilizers and movement systems before loading
- **Movement Quality Enhancement:** Mobility, stability, and coordination drills specific to upcoming activities
- **Performance Priming:** Specific warm-up protocols that enhance subsequent performance
- **Injury Prevention Integration:** Evidence-based protocols that reduce injury risk while enhancing readiness
