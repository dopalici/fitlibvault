# A38. Fatigue-Induced Movement Changes

**ID:** A38-FATIGUE-MOVEMENT-CHANGES
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Fatigue-induced movement changes involve understanding how movement patterns and mechanics alter under fatigue conditions, including assessment of movement quality, efficiency, and performance degradation.

## Key Points

- Movement quality decreases by 20-30% under fatigue
- Movement efficiency reduces by 15-25% with fatigue
- Performance output decreases by 25-35% under fatigue
- Injury risk increases by 20-40% with fatigue
- Recovery time increases by 25-35% with fatigue
