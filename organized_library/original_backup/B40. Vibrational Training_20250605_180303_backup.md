### B40. Vibrational Training
**ID:** B40-VIBRATIONAL-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Vibrational training utilizes mechanical vibrations applied to the body through specialized platforms or devices to enhance muscle activation, bone density, and neuromuscular function. This modality triggers reflexive muscle contractions and may provide benefits for strength, power, flexibility, and bone health when used as a supplement to traditional training.

**Core Components:**
- **Whole Body Vibration (WBV):** Using vibrating platforms that transmit mechanical stimuli throughout the body to enhance muscle activation and circulation
- **Localized Vibration:** Applying vibration directly to specific muscle groups or joints using handheld devices for targeted therapeutic or performance benefits
- **Frequency Optimization:** Selecting appropriate vibration frequencies (typically 15-60 Hz) based on training goals and individual response patterns
- **Amplitude and Duration Control:** Managing vibration amplitude and exposure time to maximize benefits while preventing adverse effects or habituation
- **Supplemental Integration:** Incorporating vibrational training as an adjunct to traditional exercise rather than a standalone training modality
