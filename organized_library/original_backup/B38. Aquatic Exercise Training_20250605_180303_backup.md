### B38. Aquatic Exercise Training
**ID:** B38-AQUATIC-EXERCISE
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Aquatic exercise training utilizes the unique properties of water (buoyancy, resistance, hydrostatic pressure) to provide a low-impact training environment that reduces joint stress while maintaining training intensity. This modality is particularly valuable for rehabilitation, older adults, and athletes seeking recovery-focused training sessions.

**Core Components:**
- **Buoyancy-Assisted Training:** Utilizing water's buoyancy to reduce body weight and joint loading while maintaining movement patterns and muscle activation
- **Hydrostatic Resistance:** Leveraging water's natural resistance properties for strength and cardiovascular training without external weights
- **Temperature Regulation:** Using water temperature to enhance recovery, reduce inflammation, or increase metabolic demands during training
- **Range of Motion Enhancement:** Taking advantage of water's supportive environment to improve mobility and flexibility safely
- **Progressive Loading:** Gradually increasing training intensity through depth changes, movement speed, and added resistance equipment
