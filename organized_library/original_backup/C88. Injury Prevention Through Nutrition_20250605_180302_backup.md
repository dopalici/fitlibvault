### Injury Prevention Through Nutrition
**ID:** C88 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Injury prevention through nutrition involves strategic dietary interventions designed to strengthen connective tissues, optimize bone health, reduce inflammation, and support the body's natural defense mechanisms against exercise-induced tissue damage. This proactive approach addresses nutritional factors that influence injury susceptibility and tissue resilience.

**Core Components:**
- **Connective Tissue Support:** Optimizing [[C2. Protein Requirements for Muscle Growth]] with specific amino acids and [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]] essential for collagen synthesis and tissue integrity
- **Bone Health Optimization:** Implementing targeted nutrition strategies including calcium, vitamin D, and magnesium protocols aligned with [[A41. Skeletal Adaptations to Training]]
- **Anti-Inflammatory Nutrition:** Utilizing [[C33. Anti-Inflammatory FoodsDiets]] and [[C34. Antioxidants]] to manage exercise-induced inflammation and oxidative stress
- **Hydration and Electrolyte Balance:** Maintaining optimal [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]] to prevent cramping, heat illness, and maintain neuromuscular function
- **Recovery Nutrition Integration:** Coordinating [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]] strategies with training loads to prevent cumulative fatigue and overuse injuries
