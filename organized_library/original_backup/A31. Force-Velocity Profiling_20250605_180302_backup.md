# A31. Force-Velocity Profiling

**ID:** A31-FORCE-VELOCITY-PROFILING
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Force-velocity profiling in biomechanics involves understanding the inverse relationship between force and velocity in muscle contractions, with specific training methods to optimize performance across the force-velocity spectrum.

## Key Points

- Force-velocity relationship shows inverse correlation in muscle function
- Maximum force output increases by 20-30% with strength training
- Maximum velocity improves by 15-25% with speed training
- Power output enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Performance efficiency increases by 20-40% with proper profiling

## Common Mistakes

⚠️ **Training only one end of the spectrum** - Balance is crucial for optimal function

⚠️ **Ignoring individual differences** - Each athlete requires specific attention

⚠️ **Poor exercise selection** - Exercises must match force-velocity requirements

⚠️ **Inadequate progression** - Systematic development is essential
