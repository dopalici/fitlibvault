### B33. Minimal Effective Dose Training
**ID:** B33-MINIMAL-EFFECTIVE-DOSE
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Minimal Effective Dose (MED) training focuses on identifying the smallest amount of training stimulus required to produce meaningful adaptations. This approach emphasizes efficiency over volume, seeking to maximize training benefits while minimizing time investment, fatigue accumulation, and injury risk through precise application of training variables.

**Core Components:**
- **Threshold Identification:** Determining the minimum training volume, frequency, and intensity required to maintain or improve specific fitness qualities
- **Quality over Quantity:** Prioritizing training intensity and movement quality over high-volume approaches to maximize training efficiency
- **Recovery Optimization:** Allowing maximum recovery time between sessions while maintaining training adaptations and progress
- **Time-Efficient Programming:** Designing training protocols that fit into limited time constraints while still producing meaningful results
- **Individual Threshold Assessment:** Recognizing that MED varies significantly between individuals based on training history, genetics, and recovery capacity
