### Partial Range of Motion Training
**ID:** B77 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Partial range of motion training involves deliberately limiting movement amplitude to target specific joint angles, overcome strength plateaus, or work around injuries. This technique can enhance strength at sticking points, allow heavier loading, and provide specialized training stimulus for athletic performance demands.

- **Sticking Point Training:** Targeting weak joint angles to improve overall strength using [[A7. Muscle Physiology Fundamentals]] and [[B4. Strength Training Principles]]
- **Overload Applications:** Using partial range to exceed normal loading capacity through [[B1. Progressive Overload]] and [[A19. Principle of Overload]]
- **Sport-Specific Applications:** Training movement patterns specific to athletic demands using [[A20. Principle of Specificity (SAID)]] and [[L1. Movement Patterns and Functional Training]]
