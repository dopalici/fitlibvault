### B26. Velocity-Based Training (VBT)
**ID:** B26-VELOCITY-BASED-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Velocity-Based Training (VBT) uses real-time measurement of movement velocity to prescribe and adjust training loads. This method provides objective feedback on neuromuscular performance and enables autoregulation of training intensity.

**Key Components:**
- **Movement Velocity:** Real-time measurement of concentric lifting speed
- **Load-Velocity Relationship:** Individual force-velocity profiles for load prescription
- **Velocity Zones:** Specific speed ranges targeting different adaptations
- **Autoregulation:** Daily adjustment of loads based on velocity feedback
- **Technology Integration:** Linear position transducers or accelerometer-based devices

**Training Applications:**
- Individualized load prescription based on daily readiness
- Objective monitoring of fatigue and recovery status
- Power development through optimal velocity zones
- Strength training optimization via velocity feedback
- Competition preparation and peaking protocols
