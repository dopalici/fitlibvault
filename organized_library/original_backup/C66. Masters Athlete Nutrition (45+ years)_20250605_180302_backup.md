### Masters Athlete Nutrition (45+ years)
**ID:** C66 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Masters athletes face unique nutritional challenges including decreased protein synthesis, altered hormone profiles, and slower recovery rates. Proper nutrition becomes increasingly critical for maintaining performance, bone health, and metabolic function as aging processes accelerate.

**Core Components:**
- **Enhanced Protein Requirements:** Higher protein intake (1.6-2.2g/kg) needed to combat age-related muscle loss and support recovery, with emphasis on leucine-rich sources and timing around [[B2. Protein Requirements for Muscle Growth]]
- **Micronutrient Optimization:** Increased focus on calcium, vitamin D, B12, and magnesium to support [[A41. Skeletal Adaptations to Training]] and address age-related absorption decreases
- **Anti-Inflammatory Nutrition:** Strategic use of omega-3 fatty acids and polyphenol-rich foods to combat chronic inflammation and support [[C33. Anti-Inflammatory FoodsDiets]]
- **Hydration Considerations:** Enhanced attention to fluid balance due to decreased thirst sensation and kidney function, particularly important for [[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]]
- **Metabolic Health Support:** Targeted nutrition to maintain insulin sensitivity and support [[A2. Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)]] as metabolism naturally slows
