### Pre-Exhaustion Training Systems
**ID:** B75 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Pre-exhaustion involves performing an isolation exercise immediately before a compound movement to selectively fatigue a target muscle group, forcing greater recruitment during the subsequent multi-joint exercise. This technique addresses muscle imbalances and enhances training stimulus when certain muscles limit performance in compound movements.

**Core Components:**
- **Isolation-to-Compound Sequencing:** Strategic exercise pairing to maximize target muscle activation using [[A7. Muscle Physiology Fundamentals]] and [[B3. Hypertrophy Training Principles]]
- **Muscle Recruitment Optimization:** Enhancing motor unit activation in previously limiting muscles through [[A34. Neural Adaptations to Training]] and [[A44. Neuromuscular Adaptations to Training]]
- **Exercise Selection Strategies:** Choosing appropriate isolation movements based on [[L1. Movement Patterns and Functional Training]] and [[B4. Strength Training Principles]]
- **Timing and Rest Protocols:** Managing fatigue transition between exercises using [[B6. Rest and Recovery for Optimal Adaptation (Resistance Training Focus)]] and [[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]]
- **Imbalance Correction Applications:** Addressing strength disparities and weak links incorporating [[F3. Injury Prevention and Management]] and [[A20. Principle of Specificity (SAID)]]
