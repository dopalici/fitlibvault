### Performance Nutrition Psychology
**ID:** C82 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Performance nutrition psychology examines the mental and emotional factors that influence eating behaviors, food choices, and nutritional adherence in athletic populations. This field integrates cognitive behavioral principles with nutrition science to optimize both psychological well-being and physical performance through strategic dietary interventions.

**Core Components:**
- **Cognitive Behavioral Nutrition Strategies:** Applying [[F4. Behavioral Psychology in Fitness]] principles to improve [[D2. Diet Adherence and Behavior Change]] and develop sustainable eating patterns
- **Pre-Competition Nutrition Anxiety Management:** Addressing performance-related food anxiety and developing confidence in [[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]] and competition nutrition protocols
- **Food Relationship and Performance Identity:** Understanding how athletic identity affects food choices and integrating [[C19. Intuitive Eating]] principles with performance goals
- **Stress-Eating Pattern Management:** Developing strategies from [[M3. Stress Management and Performance]] to prevent stress-induced eating behaviors that conflict with training goals
- **Mental Performance Enhancement Through Nutrition:** Using nutrition knowledge and planning as psychological tools to enhance confidence and mental resilience during training and competition
