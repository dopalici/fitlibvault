# A32. Power Profiling and Testing

**ID:** A32-POWER-PROFILING-TESTING
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Power profiling and testing in biomechanics involves understanding the relationship between force, velocity, and power output, with specific methods to assess and optimize power production across different movement patterns.

## Key Points

- Power output varies across the force-velocity spectrum
- Maximum power increases by 20-30% with targeted training
- Power endurance improves by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Movement efficiency enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Performance outcomes increase by 20-40% with systematic profiling
