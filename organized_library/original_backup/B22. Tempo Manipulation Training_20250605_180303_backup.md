### B22. Tempo Manipulation Training
**ID:** B22-TEMPO-MANIPULATION
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Tempo manipulation training involves systematically controlling the speed of movement phases during resistance exercises to enhance specific training adaptations. This method adjusts concentric, eccentric, and isometric components to target different physiological responses.

**Key Components:**
- **Eccentric Phase:** Controlled lengthening phase for increased time under tension
- **Isometric Holds:** Pauses at specific joint angles to enhance strength at sticking points
- **Concentric Speed:** Fast or controlled lifting phases for power or strength development
- **Time Under Tension:** Extended muscle activation for hypertrophy stimulus
- **Periodization:** Systematic variation of tempo across training cycles

**Training Applications:**
- Hypertrophy enhancement through increased mechanical tension
- Strength development at specific joint angles
- Power training through explosive concentric phases
- Rehabilitation and movement quality improvement
- Breaking through training plateaus
