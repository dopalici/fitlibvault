### B25. Post-Activation Potentiation (PAP)
**ID:** B25-POST-ACTIVATION-POTENTIATION
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Post-Activation Potentiation (PAP) is the acute enhancement of muscle power output following a conditioning activity involving heavy resistance or high-intensity exercise. This neurophysiological phenomenon temporarily increases subsequent explosive performance.

**Key Components:**
- **Conditioning Activity:** Heavy resistance exercise (85-95% 1RM) or explosive movements
- **Rest Interval:** Optimal recovery period (3-8 minutes) for potentiation expression
- **Target Activity:** Explosive movement performed after conditioning stimulus
- **Individual Response:** Highly variable based on training status and fiber type
- **Complex Training:** Integration of strength and power exercises in sequence

**Training Applications:**
- Pre-competition warm-up protocols for explosive sports
- Complex training methods combining strength and plyometrics
- Acute power enhancement for jumping and sprinting
- Training periodization for strength-power athletes
- Assessment of neuromuscular readiness
