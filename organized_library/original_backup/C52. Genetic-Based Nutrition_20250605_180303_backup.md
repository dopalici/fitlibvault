### C52. Genetic-Based Nutrition

**ID:** C52-GENETIC-BASED-NUTRITION **Created:** 2025-06-05

Genetic-based nutrition involves the application of genetic testing and nutrigenomic principles to personalize nutritional recommendations based on individual genetic variants. This precision approach considers how genetic polymorphisms affect nutrient metabolism, supplement responsiveness, food sensitivities, and training adaptations to optimize performance outcomes and health benefits through targeted dietary interventions.

**Core Components:**

- **Nutrigenomics Applications:** Understanding how genetic variants influence macronutrient metabolism, micronutrient absorption, and supplement efficacy to guide personalized nutrition protocols ([[K1. Genetic Factors in Athletic Performance]], [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]])
- **Metabolic Profiling:** Using genetic markers to predict individual responses to different dietary approaches including carbohydrate sensitivity, fat oxidation capacity, and protein requirements ([[A62. Metabolic Flexibility Training]], [[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]])
- **Supplement Responsiveness:** Identifying genetic variants that affect supplement absorption, metabolism, and efficacy to optimize supplementation strategies ([[C20. Supplements (Overview & Framework)]], [[C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]])
- **Food Sensitivity Management:** Addressing genetic predispositions to food intolerances, allergies, and adverse reactions through personalized dietary modifications ([[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]], [[C30. Food Quality & Health (Whole vs Processed)]])
- **Training Adaptation Support:** Integrating genetic information about recovery capacity, injury risk, and adaptation potential with nutrition strategies to enhance training outcomes ([[A35. Metabolic Adaptations to Training]], [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]])
