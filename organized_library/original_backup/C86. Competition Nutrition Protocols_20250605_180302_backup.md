### Competition Nutrition Protocols
**ID:** C86 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Competition nutrition protocols involve systematic nutritional strategies designed to optimize performance during competitive events, focusing on pre-competition preparation, during-event fueling, and immediate post-competition recovery. These protocols are sport-specific and individualized based on event duration, intensity, environmental conditions, and athlete preferences.

**Core Components:**
- **Pre-Competition Preparation:** Implementing strategic [[C17. Carb Cycling]] and [[C44. Competition Day Nutrition]] to maximize glycogen stores while minimizing gastrointestinal distress
- **During-Event Fueling:** Optimizing [[C4. Hydration]] and [[C5. Electrolytes]] replacement along with carbohydrate delivery based on exercise duration and intensity
- **Post-Competition Recovery:** Immediate application of [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]] principles to accelerate glycogen replenishment and protein synthesis
- **Event-Specific Protocols:** Tailoring nutrition strategies for different competitive formats using [[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]] and [[C28. Nutrition for Strength Power Athletes]]
- **Performance Monitoring Integration:** Utilizing [[C54. Precision Nutrition Monitoring]] and [[C77. Metabolic Testing Integration]] to refine competition nutrition strategies based on real-time feedback
