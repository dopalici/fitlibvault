### B41. Electrical Muscle Stimulation
**ID:** B41-ELECTRICAL-MUSCLE-STIMULATION
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Electrical Muscle Stimulation (EMS) involves applying electrical impulses to muscles to trigger involuntary contractions, primarily used for rehabilitation, recovery, and as a supplemental training tool. While EMS can maintain muscle mass during immobilization and assist in rehabilitation, it cannot fully replace voluntary exercise for comprehensive fitness development.

**Core Components:**
- **Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation (NMES):** Using electrical currents to stimulate motor nerves and produce muscle contractions for rehabilitation and strength maintenance
- **Recovery Enhancement:** Applying low-intensity electrical stimulation to promote blood flow, reduce muscle soreness, and accelerate recovery between training sessions
- **Targeted Muscle Activation:** Selectively stimulating specific muscles or muscle groups that may be difficult to activate voluntarily due to injury or inhibition
- **Parameter Optimization:** Adjusting stimulation frequency, intensity, pulse duration, and duty cycles based on treatment goals and individual tolerance
- **Contraindication Awareness:** Understanding safety limitations and avoiding EMS use in individuals with pacemakers, pregnancy, or certain medical conditions
