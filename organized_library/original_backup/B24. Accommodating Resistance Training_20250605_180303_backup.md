### B24. Accommodating Resistance Training
**ID:** B24-ACCOMMODATING-RESISTANCE
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Accommodating resistance training uses variable resistance that changes throughout the range of motion to match the strength curve of human movement. This method employs chains, bands, or specialized equipment to provide optimal loading at different joint angles.

**Key Components:**
- **Variable Resistance:** Load changes to match muscle's force-producing capacity
- **Strength Curves:** Accommodates ascending, descending, or bell-shaped force curves
- **Band Resistance:** Elastic bands provide increasing tension through range of motion
- **Chain Loading:** Progressive weight addition as range of motion increases
- **Cam-Based Systems:** Mechanical advantage adjustments throughout movement

**Training Applications:**
- Powerlifting performance enhancement for squat, bench, and deadlift
- Explosive power development through accommodated loading
- Overcoming strength plateaus at specific joint angles
- Injury prevention through reduced deceleration phases
- Sport-specific strength development
