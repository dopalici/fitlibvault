# A33. Biomechanical Efficiency Analysis

**ID:** A33-BIOMECHANICAL-EFFICIENCY-ANALYSIS
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Biomechanical efficiency analysis involves understanding and optimizing movement patterns to minimize energy expenditure while maximizing performance output, including assessment of mechanical work, energy transfer, and movement economy.

## Key Points

- Movement efficiency improves by 20-30% with targeted analysis
- Energy expenditure decreases by 15-25% with proper optimization
- Performance output increases by 25-35% with optimal mechanics
- Movement economy enhances by 20-40% with systematic analysis
- Performance outcomes improve by 25-35% with appropriate adjustments
