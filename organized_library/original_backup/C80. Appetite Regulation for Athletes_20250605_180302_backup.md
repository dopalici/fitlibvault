### Appetite Regulation for Athletes
**ID:** C80 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Appetite regulation for athletes involves understanding and managing hunger, satiety, and food cravings to align energy intake with training demands and body composition goals. This approach considers hormonal influences, training-induced appetite changes, and psychological factors that affect eating behaviors in athletic populations.

**Core Components:**
- **Hormonal Appetite Control:** Understanding how [[A10. Hormonal Responses to Exercise]] and [[I1. Training and Hormonal Responses]] affect leptin, ghrelin, and other appetite-regulating hormones
- **Training-Induced Appetite Suppression:** Managing post-exercise appetite changes and ensuring adequate [[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]] are met despite reduced hunger signals
- **Satiety-Enhancing Nutrition Strategies:** Utilizing protein timing, fiber intake, and food volume principles from [[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]] to optimize fullness and meal satisfaction
- **Psychological Hunger Management:** Addressing emotional eating patterns and stress-related appetite changes that may conflict with [[C26. Nutrition for Fat Loss]] or performance goals
- **Periodized Appetite Management:** Aligning appetite regulation strategies with [[A25. Principle of Periodization]] phases, including controlled hunger during cutting phases and adequate intake during building phases
