### B44. Cold Exposure Training
**ID:** B44-COLD-EXPOSURE-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Cold exposure training involves deliberate exposure to cold temperatures through cold water immersion, cryotherapy, or cold air exposure to stimulate physiological adaptations and potential performance benefits. This approach may enhance recovery, improve cold tolerance, and trigger metabolic adaptations, though timing relative to training sessions requires careful consideration.

**Core Components:**
- **Cold Water Immersion (CWI):** Using cold water baths (10-15°C) for specific durations to promote recovery, reduce inflammation, and enhance adaptation responses
- **Cryotherapy Applications:** Utilizing whole-body cryotherapy chambers or localized cryotherapy for recovery enhancement and inflammation management
- **Brown Adipose Tissue Activation:** Stimulating metabolically active brown fat through cold exposure to enhance thermogenesis and metabolic flexibility
- **Hormetic Stress Response:** Triggering beneficial stress adaptations through controlled cold exposure that may enhance resilience and recovery capacity
- **Timing Optimization:** Strategically scheduling cold exposure relative to training sessions to maximize benefits while avoiding interference with adaptation signaling
