### Gastrointestinal Training Protocols
**ID:** C60 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Gastrointestinal training protocols involve systematic conditioning of the digestive system to tolerate nutrition during exercise, particularly important for endurance athletes. These evidence-based strategies help athletes avoid GI distress while maintaining optimal fueling during prolonged or intense exercise sessions and competitions.

**Core Components:**
- **Progressive Tolerance Building:** Systematic exposure to increasing amounts and types of nutrition during exercise to build GI tolerance, building on [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] and [[E1. Aerobic vs. Anaerobic Training]]
- **Substrate-Specific Training:** Training the gut to handle different carbohydrate types, concentrations, and delivery methods during exercise, connected to [[C27. Nutrition for Endurance Performance (Carb Loading)]] and [[A6. Energy Systems (Detailed)]]
- **Microbiome Optimization:** Supporting gut health through targeted nutrition to improve exercise tolerance and nutrient absorption, linking to [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]] and [[C29. Nutrition for Recovery]]
- **Exercise Intensity Considerations:** Understanding how different exercise intensities affect GI function and adapting nutrition strategies accordingly, incorporating [[E12. Lactate Threshold Training]] and [[E3. High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)]]
- **Competition Preparation:** Specific protocols for race-day nutrition that have been tested and optimized through training, connected to [[C35. Specialized Nutrition for Performance Demands]] and [[A20. Principle of Specificity (SAID)]]
