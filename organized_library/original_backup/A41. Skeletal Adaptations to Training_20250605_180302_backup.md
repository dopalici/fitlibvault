# A41. Reactive Strength Index

**ID:** A41-REACTIVE-STRENGTH-INDEX
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Reactive Strength Index (RSI) involves understanding the relationship between jump height and ground contact time during plyometric movements, including assessment of stretch-shortening cycle efficiency, power output, and performance optimization.

## Key Points

- RSI scores improve by 20-30% with targeted training
- Power output increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Movement efficiency enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Performance outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
