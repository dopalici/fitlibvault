### Immune-Nutrition Optimization
**ID:** C63 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Immune-nutrition optimization focuses on supporting immune system function through targeted nutritional strategies, particularly important for athletes who experience exercise-induced immune suppression. This approach balances the immune-enhancing effects of moderate exercise with nutritional interventions to prevent illness and maintain consistent training availability.

**Core Components:**
- **Exercise-Induced Immunosuppression Management:** Nutritional strategies to counter the temporary immune suppression following intense training, building on [[A38. Immune System Adaptations to Training]] and [[M2. Overtraining and Functional Overreaching]]
- **Micronutrient Immune Support:** Strategic use of vitamins C, D, zinc, and other immune-critical nutrients to maintain optimal function, connected to [[C6. Micronutrients (Detailed Roles & Considerations)]] and [[C21. Evidence-Based Supplements (Group A)]]
- **Gut-Immune Axis Optimization:** Supporting immune function through gut health interventions and targeted probiotics, linking to [[C32. Gut Health and Microbiome]] and [[C33. Anti-Inflammatory FoodsDiets]]
- **Periodized Immune Support:** Adjusting nutritional immune support based on training phases and competition schedules, incorporating [[A25. Principle of Periodization]] and [[K2. Advanced Periodization Strategies]]
- **Recovery and Immune Function:** Understanding how sleep, stress management, and nutrition work together to support immune resilience, connected to [[F1. Sleep Quality and Quantity]] and [[M1. Recovery Modalities Evidence and Application]]
