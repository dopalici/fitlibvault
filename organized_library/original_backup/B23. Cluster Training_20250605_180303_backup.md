### B23. Cluster Training
**ID:** B23-CLUSTER-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Cluster training involves breaking traditional sets into smaller clusters of repetitions with short inter-repetition rest periods. This method allows for maintenance of higher intensities and power outputs throughout the training session.

**Key Components:**
- **Cluster Structure:** Small groups of reps (2-5) with 10-30 second inter-cluster rest
- **Rest Intervals:** Brief pauses to allow partial ATP-PCr replenishment
- **Load Maintenance:** Ability to use heavier loads compared to traditional sets
- **Volume Density:** Higher training density with maintained intensity
- **Power Preservation:** Reduced velocity decay across repetitions

**Training Applications:**
- Strength and power development with heavy loads
- Maintaining rep quality throughout high-volume sessions
- Concurrent training for strength-endurance athletes
- Breaking through strength plateaus
- Advanced periodization strategies
