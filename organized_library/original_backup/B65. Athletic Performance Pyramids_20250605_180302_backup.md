# B65. Athletic Performance Pyramids

**ID:** B65-PERFORMANCE-PYRAMID-DEVELOPMENT
**Created:** 2025-06-05

## Conceptual Overview

Athletic performance pyramids represent hierarchical models for developing physical capabilities, typically progressing from fundamental movement competency and general strength to sport-specific power and skill development. This systematic approach ensures that advanced training methods are built upon solid foundations of movement quality, strength, and conditioning.

## Core Components

- **Foundation Layer Development:** Movement competency, mobility, stability, and basic strength as prerequisite capabilities
- **General Strength Phase:** Development of multi-directional strength and work capacity across fundamental patterns
- **Power Development Layer:** Translation of strength into explosive power and rate of force development
- **Sport-Specific Integration:** Application of developed capabilities to sport-specific movement patterns and demands
- **Peak Performance Optimization:** Fine-tuning and specialization for competition performance
