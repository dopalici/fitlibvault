### B43. Heat Training Adaptations
**ID:** B43-HEAT-TRAINING-ADAPTATIONS
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Heat training adaptations involve deliberate exposure to elevated temperatures during exercise to improve thermoregulatory capacity and performance in hot environments. This approach triggers physiological adaptations including improved heat dissipation, plasma volume expansion, and enhanced cardiovascular efficiency that benefit both hot-weather performance and general exercise capacity.

**Core Components:**
- **Heat Acclimatization Protocol:** Gradual exposure to training in hot environments (typically 10-14 days) to develop physiological adaptations including increased plasma volume and improved sweating response
- **Thermoregulatory Enhancement:** Improving the body's ability to dissipate heat through enhanced cardiovascular function, increased sweat rate, and reduced core temperature elevation
- **Cardiovascular Adaptations:** Developing increased plasma volume, stroke volume, and cardiac output that benefit performance in both hot and temperate conditions
- **Electrolyte Management:** Optimizing fluid and electrolyte replacement strategies to support enhanced sweating capacity and maintain performance
- **Safety Monitoring:** Implementing core temperature monitoring, hydration assessment, and heat illness prevention protocols during heat training exposure
