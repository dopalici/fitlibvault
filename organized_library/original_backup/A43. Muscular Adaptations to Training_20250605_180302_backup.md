# A43. Compensatory Movement Patterns

**ID:** A43-COMPENSATORY-MOVEMENT-PATTERNS
**Created:** 2025-04-26 (Updated: 2025-01-14)

## Quick Takeaway

Compensatory movement patterns involve understanding and addressing movement adaptations that occur in response to limitations or dysfunctions, including assessment of movement strategies, efficiency, and injury prevention.

## Key Points

- Pattern recognition improves by 20-30% with targeted analysis
- Movement efficiency increases by 15-25% with proper protocols
- Performance optimization enhances by 25-35% with optimal training
- Performance outcomes improve by 20-40% with systematic practice
- Injury risk decreases by 25-35% with appropriate management
