### Restaurant Navigation for Athletes
**ID:** C73 **Created:** 2025-06-05

Athletes must maintain nutritional consistency when dining out for business, social, or travel purposes. Strategic restaurant navigation skills enable athletes to make informed choices that align with their performance goals while accommodating social and professional dining requirements.

**Core Components:**
- **Menu Analysis Skills:** Techniques for identifying dishes that align with [[C2. Macronutrient Distribution]] and [[C1. Caloric Targets for Different Goals]] despite limited nutritional information
- **Customization Strategies:** Effective communication with restaurant staff to modify dishes for optimal protein, carbohydrate, and fat ratios while maintaining palatability
- **Cuisine-Specific Guidelines:** Navigation strategies for different ethnic cuisines and cooking methods to support [[C30. Food Quality & Health (Whole vs Processed)]] principles
- **Timing Considerations:** Restaurant meal planning that integrates with [[C3. Nutrient Timing]] and [[C5. Pre, During, and Post-Exercise Nutrition]] protocols
- **Damage Control Methods:** Strategies for managing less-than-ideal food choices while minimizing impact on training adaptations and maintaining overall dietary adherence
