### B42. Hypoxic Training
**ID:** B42-HYPOXIC-TRAINING
**Created:** 2025-06-05

Hypoxic training involves exercising in reduced oxygen environments to stimulate physiological adaptations that enhance oxygen utilization efficiency and endurance performance. This approach can be achieved through altitude training, hypoxic chambers, or breathing restriction devices, triggering adaptations in oxygen transport and cellular metabolism.

**Core Components:**
- **Altitude Simulation:** Training at natural altitude or using hypoxic chambers to reduce inspired oxygen concentration and stimulate erythropoiesis
- **Intermittent Hypoxic Training (IHT):** Alternating between hypoxic and normoxic conditions during training sessions to maximize adaptation while maintaining training intensity
- **Live High, Train Low Protocol:** Residing at altitude for adaptation benefits while training at sea level to maintain high-intensity exercise capacity
- **Respiratory Training Integration:** Combining hypoxic exposure with specific breathing techniques to enhance respiratory muscle strength and efficiency
- **Individual Response Monitoring:** Tracking physiological markers (oxygen saturation, heart rate, performance metrics) to optimize hypoxic exposure and prevent altitude-related complications
